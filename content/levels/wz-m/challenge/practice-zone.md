Practice Zone is a separate training activity on Rebirth Island, introduced in
MWIII Season 5. Players test loadouts and control settings against respawning
targets; other players can join but cannot damage one another. The activity was
removed with BO6 Season 1.

It uses the supported `other` / `challenge` classification and is a canonical
activity variant of `wz-rebirth-island`, whose unchanged Warzone Mobile map
appearance is indexed separately. It reuses that map's Wiki article because no
dedicated activity article is needed.

Locations are omitted to inherit the existing Kazakhstan country fallback at
`48, 68`. That representative point is not a verified practice site or a new
claim about the island's real-world inspiration; geographic review remains
pending.
