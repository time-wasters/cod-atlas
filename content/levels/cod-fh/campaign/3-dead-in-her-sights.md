## The Mission in the Game

*Dead In Her Sights* is set in Stalingrad on October 3, 1942. The player now
controls Lieutenant Tanya Pavelovna, identified by the game as a sniper in the
13th Guards Rifle Division, with Aleksandr Sokolov acting as her spotter. From
the upper floor of a ruined building, Pavelovna times her shots with nearby
explosions, kills German troops to provoke the arrival of a Panzer, and shoots
the soldiers riding on it. A Soviet sapper then places a mine directly in the
tank's path.

After the tank is destroyed, Private Mikael Kirelenko reports that German
forces are advancing on a Soviet tank depot at the “old tractor factory.” The
group cannot cross the next street because of another tank, so it travels
through sewers, a small tunnel, and a trench before entering the factory
through a breach in its wall. The mission ends on arrival; the defence itself
continues in *Defend the Factory*. Pavelovna's personal history, the named
supporting soldiers, and this particular ambush are elements of the game's
narrative rather than identified historical participants or incidents.

## The Real Place & Differences

The “old tractor factory” is most plausibly the Dzerzhinsky Stalingrad Tractor
Factory, now known as the Volgograd Tractor Plant. It was the northernmost of
Stalingrad's great industrial complexes and had been placed under the Soviet
tank-industry administration. It manufactured and repaired armoured vehicles,
including T-34s, so the game's description of a tank depot captures part of
its wartime function. It is misleading, however, to suggest that it was merely
an obsolete civilian factory converted into a depot: it was a vast operating
industrial and military-production site.

The factory occupied a large complex rather than one building. The game's
short route through generic streets, sewers, trenches, and one hole in a wall
cannot be matched to a documented approach or workshop. Underground movement
is plausible within Stalingrad's broader “rat war,” and military research
documents movement through sewers and conduits during the later factory
fighting. No evidence was found for the specific October 3 route shown here.

Much of the plant was destroyed in the battle, rebuilt after the war, and
substantially altered again after industrial decline. The monumental main
entrance visible today dates from 1953-55, not 1942, although it provides a
stable, protected landmark at the historic plant complex.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The date corresponds to fighting on the approaches to the factory district.
On October 3, German forces were attempting to break through toward the
Stalingrad Tractor Factory. Soviet records place the 308th Rifle Division in
the Barrikady workers' settlement, the 193rd Rifle Division around the Red
October settlement, and the 39th Guards Rifle Division at the Red October
plant. The Soviet command reinforced the Tractor Factory sector with Major
General Viktor Zholudev's 37th Guards Rifle Division and the 84th Tank Brigade;
the 37th Guards crossed into Stalingrad during the night of October 2-3.

This makes the factory warning and the presence of tanks historically
plausible, but the represented formation is mismatched. Rodimtsev's 13th
Guards was principally fighting in central Stalingrad and around Mamayev
Kurgan, while the 37th Guards, 112th Rifle Division, 42nd Rifle Brigade, local
militia, and other formations defended the Tractor Factory approaches. The
similar divisional numbers should not obscure that these were different units.

The decisive German assault into the factory began on October 14, eleven days
after the mission. German armour and infantry broke through the surrounding
defences and entered the plant during extremely destructive close combat. The
game therefore anticipates and compresses the later factory battle into its
October 3 narrative. No reviewed report documents Pavelovna deliberately
provoking one Panzer, shooting its three escorts, or enabling a lone sapper to
destroy it at this location. Those events are a fictional small-unit episode
set against a genuine developing battle.
