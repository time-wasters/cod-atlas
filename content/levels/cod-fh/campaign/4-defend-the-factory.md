## The Mission in the Game

*Defend the Factory* continues from *Dead In Her Sights* at Stalingrad's
tractor factory on October 3, 1942. The player controls Lieutenant Tanya
Pavelovna, whom the game assigns to the 13th Guards Rifle Division. After
Pavelovna and her spotter Aleksandr Sokolov reach the plant through a trench,
Soviet soldiers tell them to hold it until a tank crew returns.

Pavelovna fires from a factory window while German infantry advance across the
yard. She must stop machine-gun and Panzerschreck teams from breaching the
doors. When a Panzer IV arrives and forces her away from the windows, she
covers Major Nikolai Badanov's crew as they cross the street, treats the
wounded Fyodor Belinki, and boards the factory's lone T-34 with Badanov. The
named characters and this short defence are the game's narrative, not a
documented historical action.

## The Real Place & Differences

The factory is the Dzerzhinsky Stalingrad Tractor Factory, now the Volgograd
Tractor Plant. It was a vast industrial complex in northern Stalingrad rather
than the single, compact building implied by the level, and it manufactured
and repaired T-34 tanks as well as tractors and other military equipment.

Some details have genuine counterparts. Volgograd's regional heritage register
identifies the plant administration as the 1942 factory-defence headquarters,
Shop No. 5 as a place where volunteer worker tank crews formed, and the
cast-iron foundry as a combat site of the 37th Guards Rifle Division. These
records make the combination of factory defenders and tank crews historically
plausible, but no evidence connects the level's window, doors, yard, or lone
T-34 to any one of those locations.

The wartime plant was devastated and later rebuilt. Its present monumental
entrance dates from 1953–55, and the T-34 displayed in front is a memorial.
They mark the correct complex but should not be read as surviving scenery from
Pavelovna's fictional defence.

## The Real Mission & Differences

On October 3, German forces were pressing toward the Tractor Factory district,
while the Soviet command reinforced that sector with the 37th Guards Rifle
Division and 84th Tank Brigade. The 37th Guards had begun crossing the Volga
during the night of October 2–3. The game's factory alarm therefore fits the
broad situation, but its unit attribution does not: Rodimtsev's 13th Guards
was fighting farther south in central Stalingrad and around Mamayev Kurgan.

The principal German assault into the Tractor Factory began on October 14.
The 37th Guards, 112th Rifle Division, 42nd Rifle Brigade, factory militia, and
other Soviet formations fought in and around the plant. The mission compresses
that later factory battle into October 3 and reduces a large, destructive
combined-arms action to one sniper covering one returning tank crew.

![Map: The LI Army Corps' assault, 14–15 October 1942](the-li-army-corps-assault-14-15-october-1942.png)

The Panzerschreck teams are an additional anachronism. German development of
that weapon followed the capture of American bazookas in North Africa in
spring 1943, so it could not have appeared at Stalingrad in 1942. Major
Nikolai Badanov is also a game character. His name and later role echo the real
General Vasily Badanov, whose 24th Tank Corps raided Tatsinskaya in December
1942, but no reviewed evidence places that commander in this factory defence
or identifies the game's lone T-34 episode as a real event.
