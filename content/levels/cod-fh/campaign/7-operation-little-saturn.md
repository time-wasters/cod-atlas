## The Mission in the Game

*Operation Little Saturn* is set at Tatsinskaya on December 24, 1942. The
player controls Major Nikolai Badanov of the game's 24th Tank Corps. The
opening narration connects the attack to the encirclement at Stalingrad and
orders the corps to seize the Luftwaffe airfield, destroy aircraft, and disrupt
the supply of the trapped German Sixth Army.

The mission begins north of the airfield perimeter. A German air attack forces
Badanov's T-34 group to advance before its supporting rocket launchers are
ready. The tanks fight through antiaircraft guns, armour, infantry, barracks,
and warehouses. After helping other Soviet tank crews and receiving a rocket
barrage, Badanov dismounts to clear anti-tank troops from a warehouse. The
mission ends as the repaired tanks prepare to enter the airfield; the runway,
aircraft, and control building are attacked in the following mission,
*Airfield Ambush* (`cod-fh-airfield-ambush`).

## The Real Place & Differences

Tatsinskaya Airfield was a real military airfield south of the stanitsa of
Tatsinskaya, about 260 kilometres west of Stalingrad. By late 1942 it was the
Luftwaffe's principal external base for Junkers Ju 52 transports supplying the
encircled Sixth Army. The airfield site still exists, although it is no longer
the wartime German transport hub.

The game's broad setting—open winter steppe, an airfield compound, warehouses,
antiaircraft positions, and departing aircraft—fits the historical target. No
reviewed source, however, matches its gates, towers, connected warehouses, or
short combat route to a documented 1942 site plan. “German Airfield 32,” used
for the continuation mission, is a game designation rather than a verified
wartime name for Tatsinskaya.

## The Real Mission & Differences

This level is based on a genuine part of Operation Little Saturn. The wider
offensive began on December 16, and the real 24th Tank Corps started crossing
the Don on December 17. It then drove deep through the Axis rear, cleared
Skosyrskaya on December 23, and reached Tatsinskaya before dawn on December 24.
The game's date is therefore correct for the airfield attack, but its immediate
start at the perimeter omits a week-long armoured advance of roughly 240
kilometres.

The real corps was commanded by Major General Vasily Mikhailovich Badanov. No
reviewed evidence identifies the game's Major Nikolai Badanov or his named crew
as participants. The historical assault was also larger and more coordinated
than the player's tank group: the 4th Guards Tank Brigade attacked around the
northern edge of Tatsinskaya, the 54th Tank Brigade pressed from the west, and
the 130th Tank Brigade cut the railway and sent the battalions that stormed the
airfield on the settlement's southern side.

The opening narration says the Germans had discovered the attack and begun a
frantic evacuation. Permission to evacuate was indeed given on December 23,
and many aircraft escaped once Soviet tanks appeared. Nevertheless, the
Stalingrad Battle Museum-Reserve describes the actual dawn deployment under
fog as a successful tactical surprise. The game's failed-surprise premise is
therefore only partly accurate.

Real resistance included German forces and Romanian antiaircraft units; the
game presents the defenders simply as Wehrmacht. Rocket support is plausible
for a Soviet tank corps, but no reviewed account confirms the mission's exact
delayed-barrage sequence. Its Panzerschreck teams are anachronistic because
that German weapon was developed after captured American bazookas were studied
in 1943.

Sources give sharply different aircraft totals and do not always count the
same categories. The Museum-Reserve repeats high Soviet claims that include
captured and dismantled aircraft, whereas accounts based on Luftwaffe strength
give a loss of roughly 70 of 180 Ju 52s. The secure conclusion is that the raid
overran the base, destroyed or captured substantial aircraft and ground
equipment, and interrupted the airlift—not one exact total. The real corps was
then isolated, defended the area, and broke out on December 28, a difficult
aftermath omitted from the game's Soviet campaign.

## Marker Position Explanation

The marker at `48.16722, 41.27778` is placed on the documented Tatsinskaya
Airfield site. This is both the level's explicit target and the central
historical objective of the 24th Tank Corps' raid. `precision: exact`,
`confidence: high`, and `method: verified-landmark` apply to the verified
airfield footprint; they do not claim that a particular in-game warehouse,
gate, or tank position survives at this coordinate.

The Tatsinskaya “Breakthrough” memorial northeast of the settlement is a strong
commemorative alternative, but it is about five kilometres from the settlement
and does not mark the airfield itself. The actual target is therefore the more
accurate mission marker.
