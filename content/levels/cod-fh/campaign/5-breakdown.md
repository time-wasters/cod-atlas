## The Mission in the Game

*Breakdown* is set in Stalingrad on October 3, 1942, immediately after *Defend
the Factory*. The player controls tank commander Nikolai Badanov, whom the game
assigns to the 24th Tank Corps. His crew's T-34 develops engine trouble shortly
after escaping the tractor factory, forcing it to stop in a large square while
Sergeant Fyodor Belinki makes repairs. Badanov fights German infantry during an
air raid; a Panzer then fires on Tanya Pavelovna's position before another
Soviet T-34 destroys it.

Once mobile, the crew follows another T-34 through generic city streets,
fighting infantry and Panzer IVs on the way to General Leonid Belov's
headquarters beside the Volga. After defending the headquarters, Badanov
receives a replacement radio for forward observers in a railway-station tower
across Red Square, setting up *Into Red Square*. The level does not name its
repair square, streets, headquarters building, or route.

The opening narration calls the factory an old plant in “Volgograd.” That is a
minor but clear anachronism: the city was named Stalingrad in 1942 and did not
become Volgograd until 1961.

## The Real Place & Differences

The mission begins in the northern Tractor Factory district, but its subsequent
journey is a composite. The actual Stalingrad Tractor Factory was a vast
industrial complex, and the city extended for many kilometres along the Volga.
No reviewed evidence connects the game's open repair square, short street loop,
riverfront headquarters, or railway-station route to a reproducible path on a
wartime map.

A documented armoured battlefield lies immediately west of the factory.
Volgograd's regional heritage register identifies Opolchenskaya Street 8 as a
place where the 6th Guards Separate Tank Brigade fought from October 1 to 15,
1942. This is the closest date-and-place match for the level's T-34 combat, but
it is a historical counterpart rather than a confirmed model for the map.

The modern address is occupied by post-war development, and the surrounding
district and factory were extensively rebuilt after the battle. Its present
street plan and buildings therefore should not be compared literally with the
level's square and ruins.

## The Real Mission & Differences

On October 3, fighting was intensifying on the approaches to the northern
factory district. The 37th Guards Rifle Division was being transferred into
Stalingrad, while real Soviet tank brigades supported the 62nd Army. The
registered 6th Guards Tank Brigade site demonstrates that Soviet armour was
fighting near the Tractor Factory during the mission's date range. Field
repairs to a T-34 under difficult conditions are broadly credible, but no
reviewed account documents Belinki's repair, Pavelovna's wounding, or this
crew's drive to General Belov.

The game's formation is wrong for the city fighting. The real 24th Tank Corps,
commanded by Major General Vasily Badanov, had fought west of Stalingrad on the
Don and later rebuilt for offensive operations. Its documented association
with the Battle of Stalingrad is the deep raid on Tatsinskaya in December 1942,
not service as a small tank detachment inside the city on October 3. The game
combines that real commander and corps with the experiences of other Soviet
armoured units actually defending Stalingrad.

The chronology is compressed in other ways. The principal German assault into
the Tractor Factory began on October 14, and no evidence was found for a Soviet
headquarters operation matching Belov's radio-delivery order. The German tanks
and air attacks fit the broader battle, but the Panzerschreck-equipped infantry
do not: that weapon was developed only after German forces captured American
bazookas in North Africa in 1943.
