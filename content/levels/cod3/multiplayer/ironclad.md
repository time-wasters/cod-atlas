## The Mission in the Game

*Ironclad* is a multiplayer map rather than a campaign mission, so it has no
playable character, narrative route, mission date, or historical objective. The
Call of Duty Wiki places the American-versus-German map in Saint-Malo, France,
and gives its console codename as `shipyard`. Call of Duty Maps likewise lists
Saint-Malo and describes the playable area as lying between two large ships
under construction, with tunnels used as flank routes.

Treyarch level designer Jason McCord gives stronger environmental context:
he describes *Ironclad* as a large dry-dock map with giant ships being built and
a train wreck in the center. He says he designed it for the Xbox 360 Valor DLC
while the rest of the team was moving toward *World at War*. No source found
during this research identifies *Ironclad* as a conversion of, or asset reuse
from, a *Call of Duty 3* campaign mission, so no campaign-level relationship is
recorded.

## The Real Place & Differences

Saint-Malo is a historic port city in Brittany. During the Second World War it
formed part of the German Atlantic Wall fortress system, and the port and city
became a major battlefield in August 1944. That real maritime and industrial
setting is compatible with the map's shipyard theme, but the available game
sources identify only the city; they do not name a specific Saint-Malo dock,
shipyard, basin, or wartime industrial facility as the model for *Ironclad*.

A seemingly stronger candidate is the documented industrial site on quai
Garnier-Dufougeray, because the heritage record describes a shipbuilding complex
with a dry dock and railway access. It should not be treated as an exact match,
however: the same record says that in 1944 this site was occupied by the
La Morue Bretonne cod-drying works, which was about 95 percent destroyed that
year, while the later shipbuilding use belongs to the post-war history of the
site. The resemblance therefore does not justify moving the marker from
city-level Saint-Malo to that exact dock.

## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no in-game mission to match against a specific historical operation.
The closest supported wartime context is the Battle of Saint-Malo. U.S. Army
forces of the 83rd Infantry Division fought to reduce the German fortress in
August 1944; the official U.S. Army history records that German demolitions
destroyed the port's quays, locks, breakwaters, and harbor machinery on
7 August. Saint-Malo's municipal memorial history likewise identifies the
83rd Division as the force assigned to liberate the sector.

This context makes American-versus-German combat in a Saint-Malo port setting
historically plausible in a broad sense, but no evidence was found for a real
battle specifically corresponding to *Ironclad*'s dry dock, ships under
construction, tunnels, and train wreck. It should therefore remain a standalone
multiplayer setting rather than being attached to one of the Normandy-breakout
campaign missions.
