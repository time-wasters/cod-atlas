## The Mission in the Game

*Crossing* is a multiplayer map in *Call of Duty 3*, released with the Valor Map Pack on 27 January 2007. As a multiplayer map it has **no campaign mission narrative**, playable campaign character, historical date, briefing, route or historical objective. The Call of Duty Wiki identifies the teams as American and German, gives the location as **Le Merderet, France**, and lists the console codename `mp_isle`.

The map is documented as a conversion of the singleplayer mission *The Island* (`cod3-the-island`). The relationship is therefore evidence-based rather than inferred from visual similarity: the Wiki explicitly names *The Island* as the campaign map and says *Crossing* is part of a conversion from it. However, the multiplayer setting is deliberately re-labelled. *The Island* is set at Saint-Germain-sur-Sèves and represents the Sèves marsh battlefield, while *Crossing* is described as a bridge over the **Merderet** in Normandy. The shared relationship is primarily one of converted combat space/assets, not identical real-world geography.

In the multiplayer description, German fortifications occupy one side of the river and the Allied side uses a farmhouse as its base. Call of Duty Maps likewise describes a large open farm with few buildings, long sight lines and additional buildings and a church visible outside the playable area. These are useful environmental clues, but they do not identify a unique historical bridge.

## The Real Place & Differences

The **Merderet** is a real river in the Cotentin peninsula of Normandy. During the Normandy landings, the 82nd Airborne Division's objectives in the Merderet sector included capturing and holding the river crossings at **La Fière** and **Chef-du-Pont**. The Airborne Museum in Sainte-Mère-Église identifies both bridges as explicit 82nd Airborne objectives.

Of those two documented crossings, **Pont de la Fière** is the strongest real-world analogue for *Crossing*. U.S. Army official history describes La Fière as one of the two known Merderet crossings in the division's zone and emphasizes its exposed causeway across the flooded river flats. The Cotentin tourism authority identifies the surviving La Fière bridge as a strategically important Merderet crossing fought over by American airborne troops and German forces, with the manor beside the river forming part of the battlefield.

This remains a **plausible inspiration/analogue**, not a confirmed real location. Neither the game sources nor an official developer source found in this research names La Fière specifically. **Chef-du-Pont is the principal rejected alternative**: it was the other major 82nd Airborne bridge objective on the Merderet and therefore also fits the multiplayer location label. La Fière is preferred because the surviving bridge, causeway, adjacent manor/farm landscape and well-documented prolonged fight around the crossing provide the strongest modern battlefield anchor for the map's broad “bridge plus farm/fortifications” concept. The map's individual farmhouse, church sightline, trenches, fortifications and playable dimensions should not be treated as a surveyed reconstruction of La Fière.

The campaign relationship does **not** justify using the marker from *The Island*. The curated *The Island* level is anchored at the Stèle de Sèves Island in Saint-Germain-sur-Sèves, approximately twenty kilometres south of La Fière, because that campaign mission represents the 90th Infantry Division's July 1944 fighting in the Sèves marshes. *Crossing* reuses the campaign space but explicitly changes its named geography to the Merderet.

## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no historical mission corresponding directly to the multiplayer match because *Crossing* has no campaign date, unit narrative or scripted objective. The closest supported historical context is the battle for the **Merderet crossings during Operation Neptune on and after 6 June 1944**.

The Airborne Museum states that the 82nd Airborne Division was tasked with taking Sainte-Mère-Église and capturing and holding the bridges at La Fière and Chef-du-Pont while establishing a bridgehead west of the Merderet. U.S. Army official history describes La Fière as a narrow, exposed causeway and records repeated attempts to seize and hold the crossing amid strong German resistance. The Cotentin tourism authority likewise records German counterattacks after the bridge was taken and continued fighting on 7 June.

That D-Day context should be kept separate from *The Island*. The campaign mission depicts the **90th Infantry Division at Saint-Germain-sur-Sèves in July 1944**, while the historical La Fière battle involved airborne forces in the Merderet sector on D-Day. The multiplayer conversion therefore combines assets inherited from a later Sèves campaign level with a different named Normandy river setting. No evidence found supports transferring Pvt. Nichols, the 90th Infantry Division, *The Island*'s July date, or its campaign objectives to *Crossing*.
