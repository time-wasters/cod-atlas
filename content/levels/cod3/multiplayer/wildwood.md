## The Real Place & Differences

**Match classification: confirmed city-level setting, with Côte 314 as a plausible historical analogue for the map's hilltop listening-post concept.**

Mortain, now part of the commune of Mortain-Bocage in Normandy, was the focal point of the German counter-offensive known as Operation Lüttich in August 1944. Immediately east of the town rises **Côte 314 (Hill 314 / La Montjoie)**. Normandy Tourism identifies the hill, its Petite Chapelle Saint-Michel and the surrounding Montjoie rocks as a major site of the Battle of Mortain from 6–12 August 1944.

Côte 314 is a particularly strong analogue for Wildwood because the real position combined exactly the kinds of features suggested by the map's fiction: elevated observation, wooded ground and views over roads and countryside. The National WWII Museum records that American forward artillery observers on Hill 314 could watch German movements on the roads below and direct artillery fire by radio; it also describes the defended summit as a small wooded enclave. This fits Wildwood's “mountaintop listening post” premise far better than a generic point in Mortain.

The match is nevertheless **inferred, not confirmed**. ## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no in-game Wildwood mission to match to a real operation. The closest supported historical context is the **Battle of Mortain / Operation Lüttich**.

The U.S. 30th Infantry Division took over the Mortain sector shortly before the German counterattack of 7 August 1944. The National Guard's historical account identifies Hill 314 as key terrain and places the 2nd Battalion, 120th Infantry Regiment there. The National WWII Museum likewise records that several companies of the battalion were surrounded east of Mortain, where roughly 700 Americans held the hill until relief on 12 August. Forward observers from the 230th Field Artillery Battalion used the height to direct artillery against German forces below.

Those events provide a convincing historical context for an Allied hilltop observation/listening-post map at Mortain, but they do **not** establish Wildwood as a simulation of the Hill 314 defense. Wildwood has no stated date, named historical unit or Operation Lüttich objective, so the real battle should be treated as the closest documented analogue rather than the map's canonical mission history.
