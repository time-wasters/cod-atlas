## The Map in the Game

**Wildwood** is a *Call of Duty 3* multiplayer map released with the Valor Map Pack. Both the Call of Duty Wiki and Call of Duty Maps identify its location as **Mortain, France**.

The Call of Duty Wiki describes the map as a **mountaintop listening post for the Allies** and gives the console codename **`mp_hilltops`**. The playable area is dominated by grass, trees and dense vegetation, with tents and sniper guard towers at several positions. Call of Duty Maps likewise describes long views over surrounding fields, farmhouses and large hills. The official Valor Map Pack description from Xbox summarized Wildwood as a fight through the thick trees and overgrowth of a **mountaintop listening post**.

These details make the existing France-wide fallback much too coarse: the game sources do not merely identify the country, but independently name **Mortain** and explicitly place the action on high ground.

## The Real Place & Historical Fit

**Match classification: Mortain is confirmed; Hill 314 is a strong but still inferential fit for the specific multiplayer setting.**

Mortain was the focal point of the German **Operation Lüttich** counterattack in August 1944. Immediately east of the town is the prominent high ground commonly called **Hill 314 / Côte 314**, associated with **La Montjoie**. During the Battle of Mortain, elements of the U.S. 30th Infantry Division held this height while surrounded by German forces.

The historical terrain matches Wildwood unusually well. U.S. Army artillery material describing the battle says forward observers on Hill 314 could see for miles across **fields and trees, with occasional hedgerows and farm buildings**. The National WWII Museum likewise describes the preserved summit area as a vantage point from which American forward observers directed artillery against German movements below. Historical accounts of the defense also describe listening posts around the American perimeter.

There was additionally a wartime observation position on the La Montjoie rocks near Hill 314. D-Day Overlord identifies surviving remains of a German observation post there and emphasizes the commanding view over the valleys and roads around Mortain. The hill therefore provides a real historical counterpart for the game's fictionalized listening-post / sniper-tower concept without requiring the multiplayer layout to reproduce a specific surviving structure.

No reviewed source states explicitly that Treyarch modeled Wildwood on Hill 314, and recognizable real-world structures such as the Petite Chapelle Saint-Michel are not required by the multiplayer layout. The hill identification should therefore remain an informed geographic reconstruction rather than a confirmed one-to-one map recreation.
