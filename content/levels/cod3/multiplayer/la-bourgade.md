## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no documented historical mission corresponding specifically to a multiplayer round on La Bourgade. The closest supported historical context is the **90th Infantry Division's advance on and entry into Périers on 27 July 1944**, during the breakout phase of the Normandy campaign.

The U.S. Army Center of Military History's *Breakout and Pursuit* records that, before the renewed VIII Corps attack, the 90th Division faced the Sèves floodplain and the German-held Saint-Germain area. The division was ordered to bypass Saint-Germain on both sides and advance on Périers. On 26 July the 359th Infantry crossed the Sèves and fought through marshy ground while the 357th Infantry attacked through the Carentan-Périers isthmus. During the night German forces withdrew. On 27 July the 90th Division reconnaissance troop moved ahead of the 359th Infantry; a destroyed bridge and extensive minefields delayed the advance, but by mid-afternoon the reconnaissance element had entered the badly battered, largely deserted town of Périers. Fighting continued south of town against German delaying forces and tanks.

This history connects Périers directly to the same 90th Infantry Division represented in the adjacent *Call of Duty 3* campaign sequence, but the game's multiplayer map should not be read as a reconstruction of that operation. The game does not provide a date, named unit, historical objective, or scenario for La Bourgade. Likewise, there is no evidence that its vehicle combat or house-to-house multiplayer action reproduces the actual sequence of the 27 July advance. The historical record instead emphasizes German withdrawal, minefields, damaged bridges, reconnaissance, and delaying actions. The relationship to **The Island** is therefore best described as geographic and chronological context, not a documented asset or combat-space relationship.
