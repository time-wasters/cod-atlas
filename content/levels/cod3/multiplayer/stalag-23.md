## The Mission in the Game

Stalag 23 has no mission narrative. It is a *Call of Duty 3* multiplayer map for American-versus-German combat, set in an abandoned prisoner-of-war camp **near Metz, France**. Contemporary Xbox material likewise describes it simply as an abandoned prison camp, while Call of Duty Maps places it at Metz and describes a compound of barracks, alleyways and guard towers with wooded and agricultural terrain visible beyond the perimeter.

The map is documented as a remake of the original *Call of Duty* multiplayer map **POW Camp** (`cod-pow-camp`). POW Camp in turn adapts the compound from the campaign mission **Dulag IIIA** (`cod-dulag-iiia`). That lineage matters for the layout, but not for the CoD3 geography: POW Camp/Dulag IIIA are tied to Strasshof an der Nordbahn, Austria, whereas Stalag 23 is explicitly relocated to the Metz area.

## The Real Place & Differences

Metz is a real city in Moselle, now in France's Grand Est region. During the Second World War, Moselle was de facto annexed to Germany and incorporated into Gau Westmark, so the game's simple label “France” reflects the modern geographic location rather than the wartime administrative situation.

There was a genuine German POW installation in Metz: Fort de Queuleu was used after the 1940 defeat as Frontstalag 212 and then Stalag XII E. However, the documented Metz camp designation does not match the fictional title “Stalag 23,” its POW-camp phase belongs mainly to 1940–41, and no reviewed game source identifies Fort de Queuleu as Treyarch's model. The fort is therefore useful historical context, not a defensible exact marker for the multiplayer map.

## The Real Mission & Differences

No specific historical operation is documented for Stalag 23. The closest major wartime combat context is the Battle of Metz, fought by the U.S. Third Army against German forces in late 1944, but the multiplayer map does not identify itself as a recreation of that battle or of a particular POW-camp assault.

The inherited POW Camp/Dulag IIIA layout should also not move the marker to Austria. Those earlier maps are already curated around the former Strasshof camp grounds, while Stalag 23 deliberately changes the stated setting to Metz. The remake relationship is therefore architectural/gameplay lineage rather than shared canonical geography.
