## The Mission in the Game

Eder Dam is a standalone multiplayer map in *Call of Duty 3*, so it has no mission narrative, playable campaign character, unit chronology, briefing, or historical objective. The Call of Duty Wiki identifies the teams simply as Americans versus Germans and gives the setting as Hesse in the Kellerwald mountain range, Germany. It describes a large, open battlefield organized around a river, a dam, a partially destroyed bridge, wooded slopes, scattered buildings and bunkers, with vehicles and long sight lines playing an important role.

The Wiki explicitly lists its `Campaign Map` as `None`. It therefore should not be presented as a multiplayer conversion of a *Call of Duty 3* campaign level. There is nevertheless a cross-game geographic relationship to the original *Call of Duty* campaign mission **The Eder Dam** (`cod-the-eder-dam`), which is also set at the real Edertalsperre. The evidence supports a shared real-world place, not a claim that the *Call of Duty 3* map reuses the earlier mission's assets, route, combat space, or narrative.

## The Real Place & Differences

The best-supported classification is **confirmed real location**. The named Eder Dam corresponds to the Edertalsperre at Edersee in the municipality of Edertal, Hesse. The official Edersee tourism authority identifies the surviving masonry dam as a roughly 400-metre structure built from 1908 to 1914, and the German Federal Waterways and Shipping Administration operates the `EDERTALSPERRE` gauge on the Eder at this site. The dam stands in the wooded, hilly landscape of northern Hesse at the edge of the Kellerwald-Edersee region, which is consistent with the broad environmental description attached to the multiplayer map.

The match should not be extended into a one-to-one reconstruction claim. The multiplayer arena emphasizes a giant partially destroyed bridge, combat-ready bunkers and buildings, vehicle routes and deliberately open firing lanes. No evidence reviewed establishes that this playable layout reproduces the real dam complex at survey accuracy, or that the damaged bridge in the middle of the map represents a specific documented wartime bridge at Edertalsperre. The real landmark is secure; the detailed multiplayer battlefield around it is fictionalized for gameplay.

## The Real Mission & Differences

Because this is a standalone multiplayer map, there is no documented real ground mission corresponding to an American-versus-German match at Eder Dam. The strongest historical context is instead the actual attack on the dam during Operation Chastise. On the night of 16-17 May 1943, RAF No. 617 Squadron used Barnes Wallis's Upkeep weapons against German dams; the Eder Dam was breached. The RAF Museum documents the Eder as one of the principal targets and preserves target photographs and material from the raid.

That history differs sharply from the multiplayer scenario. Operation Chastise was an RAF bombing operation, not an American ground battle, and the game gives the multiplayer map no date or historical objective. The original *Call of Duty* mission **The Eder Dam** places British SAS characters at the repaired dam on 2 September 1944 in a fictional sabotage operation, but the *Call of Duty 3* multiplayer page does not identify that mission as its campaign source. The shared landmark therefore supplies useful geographic context only; it does not turn the multiplayer fighting into a documented 1943 or 1944 engagement.
