## The Mission in the Game

`Eder Dam` is a standalone multiplayer map rather than a campaign mission. The Call of Duty Wiki places it in Hesse in the Kellerwald mountain range, Germany, and explicitly lists its campaign map as `None`. The playable area is built around a dam and river in a wooded mountain valley, with a damaged bridge through the middle, concrete buildings, vehicle routes, and long sightlines. Call of Duty Maps likewise describes a large dam on a mountain ridge in Germany.

There is nevertheless a documented campaign connection elsewhere in the series. The Call of Duty Wiki identifies Eder Dam as a location appearing in both the original *Call of Duty* and *Call of Duty 3*. The original game uses the same real landmark for the singleplayer level `The Eder Dam` (`cod-the-eder-dam`), whose existing curated atlas record is already fixed to the Edertalsperre. This is a shared real-world setting across games, not evidence that the *Call of Duty 3* multiplayer map was converted from a *Call of Duty 3* campaign level or reused that campaign level's playable geometry.

## The Real Place & Differences

The strongest real-world match is the Edertalsperre, also known in English as the Edersee Dam, in Edertal, Hesse. The surviving gravity dam spans the Eder at the northern edge of the Kellerwald and is about 400 metres long. That combination of name, state, mountain-region context, dam, and river matches the multiplayer metadata unusually closely, so this is a confirmed landmark identification rather than a generic German-dam analogue.

The multiplayer layout remains a gameplay adaptation. Its damaged bridge, road network, bunkers and concrete buildings should not be treated as a scale reconstruction of the real dam complex, and the map sources do not establish that every structure or combat route corresponds to a specific real feature.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The map itself has no dated operation to compare with a historical ground battle. The closest major wartime event tied to the real landmark is Operation Chastise: on 16–17 May 1943, No. 617 Squadron RAF attacked the Möhne, Eder and Sorpe dams, and the Eder Dam was breached. The RAF Museum preserves target material specifically prepared for the Eder Dam in May 1943.

The original *Call of Duty* campaign later places its fictional SAS mission `The Eder Dam` at the rebuilt dam on 2 September 1944. That campaign mission helps confirm which real landmark the series means by “Eder Dam”, but no reviewed source assigns the *Call of Duty 3* multiplayer battle a date, unit history, or separate real firefight at the site. Its combat should therefore not be presented as a recreation of Operation Chastise or of the fictional 1944 campaign raid.
