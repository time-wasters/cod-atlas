## The Mission in the Game

*Fuel Plant* is a multiplayer map, so it has no mission narrative of its own. The Call of Duty Wiki places the map in **Autun, France** and explicitly describes it as being based on the singleplayer *Fuel Plant* mission (`cod3-fuel-plant`). The multiplayer version adapts the same German-controlled industrial setting into a night map, with elevated catwalks, a small rail yard, large industrial structures and motorcycles. Call of Duty Maps independently lists the multiplayer location as Autun.

The linked campaign mission provides the relevant narrative context: on **August 9, 1944**, James Doyle and other British special-operations personnel work with the French Resistance to penetrate and destroy a German-held fuel plant near Autun. That relationship is documented by the game sources and is much stronger evidence for this map's location than visual similarity to an unrelated industrial site would be.

## The Real Place & Differences

Autun is a defensible city-level identification, but a much more specific historical analogue exists immediately north of the city. The **Mine des Télots** at Saint-Forgeot, in the Autun oil-shale basin, included a distillation plant and refinery that produced automotive fuel. Government mining research records the installation of a new refinery at Télots in the 1936-1945 period and notes that, in 1942, the operator planned a major increase in shale and hydrocarbon production. Modern government planning documents locate the former Télots industrial site in Saint-Forgeot, within the Autun living area, and record surviving industrial ruins and the site's two prominent spoil heaps.

This is not evidence that the multiplayer geometry is a one-to-one reconstruction of Télots. The game presents a compact, intact combat arena with large catwalks, rail infrastructure and purpose-built routes, while the historical complex was a much larger mining, distillation and refinery site and was later partly demolished. No developer source was found explicitly naming Mine des Télots as the map's model.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The closest documented historical parallel is the **Scullion** sabotage effort against the Télots refinery. British Special Operations Executive personnel attempted to attack the strategically important refinery in 1943, and the site was associated with resistance activity during the occupation. This creates an unusually close match to the campaign premise behind the multiplayer map: a British special-operations attack on a German-used fuel facility in the Autun area, with French resistance involvement in the broader local struggle.

The chronology is nevertheless different. The game places its *Fuel Plant* mission on **August 9, 1944**, whereas the Scullion operations against Télots took place in **1943**. The game's characters, assault sequence and destruction of the plant are therefore best treated as fictionalized or composite material rather than a recreation of one documented 1944 action.
