## The Mission in the Game

Dead Ops Arcade is an unlockable top-down, twin-stick Zombies mode in *Call of
Duty: Black Ops*. Players have three lives and unlimited ammunition as they
fight through 40 rounds, collect weapons, treasure, and power-ups, and
ultimately confront the Cosmic Silverback. Its progression teleports players
through ten differently themed arenas: Island, Courtyard, Prison, Cave,
Marketplace, Urban, Street, Facility, Forest, and Tropical Forest.
