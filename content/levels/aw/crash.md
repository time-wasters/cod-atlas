## The Mission in the Game

“Crash” is the ninth singleplayer mission in *Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare*. Jack Mitchell operates with the fictional Sentinel Task Force against Atlas Corporation on January 3, 2060. The mission is a direct continuation of “Sentinel”: Sentinel has tracked an Atlas cargo aircraft, Pandora, carrying the fictional Manticore bioweapon toward Rio Gallegos Air Base in Argentina and plans to force it down at an interception point identified as Darwin Glacier, Antarctica.

The game sources disagree slightly on the clock time. The mission article infobox gives 1730 HRS, while the transcript reproduces the gameplay title card as 1750 HRS. Both agree on January 3, 2060 and on Antarctica, and the transcript explicitly says the interception point is marked at Darwin Glacier.

Mitchell and Cormack board the cargo aircraft in flight and bring it down, after which Sentinel attempts to recover the Manticore cargo. Atlas reinforcements attack, and the route moves through wreckage, a ravine, ice caves and water, mountain or glacier ledges, and open snowfields before a final fight around the cargo. Gideon defects from Atlas, Sentinel shoots down an Atlas Warbird, and an avalanche partly buries the cargo. These events, organizations, weapons, and objectives are fictional rather than a reconstruction of a historical Antarctic operation.

## The Real Place & Differences

**Classification: confirmed real location at the named-feature level; fictionalized mission route and action.** Darwin Glacier is a real, officially named Antarctic glacier. The SCAR Composite Gazetteer entry hosted by the Australian Antarctic Data Centre describes it as a large glacier flowing east from the polar plateau between the Darwin and Cook Mountains to the Ross Ice Shelf. The same record gives a gazetteer reference position of 79°53′S, 159°00′E.

That broad geography makes the game's combination of glacier ice, exposed mountain terrain and steep relief plausible as an Antarctic visual setting. However, no evidence was found that the developers reproduced a particular real crash site, cave system, ravine, ledge route, or avalanche path on Darwin Glacier. The playable space should therefore be treated as a dramatized combat environment placed on a genuine named glacier, not as a surveyable one-to-one reconstruction. DLR remote-sensing imagery confirms the real Darwin Glacier's extensive ice-and-mountain landscape but does not establish a match to the level's detailed layout.

## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no matching real military mission. “Crash” is set in 2060 and involves the fictional Sentinel Task Force, Atlas Corporation and Manticore, so its aircraft interception, firefight, orbital reinforcement, cargo recovery and avalanche sequence have no historical counterpart to identify at Darwin Glacier.

The closest documented historical context is exploration and scientific work. The SCAR/AADC gazetteer states that the lower glacier was mapped during the British National Antarctic Expedition of 1901–04 and that New Zealand parties of the Commonwealth Trans-Antarctic Expedition traversed the wider area in 1956–58. Modern Antarctic activity is primarily scientific and logistical. Under the Antarctic Treaty as it exists today, Antarctica is reserved for peaceful purposes and measures of a military nature are prohibited, while military personnel or equipment may still be used for scientific research or other peaceful purposes. That present-day treaty context contrasts with the level's open combat, but it should not be read as a claim about the fictional legal situation in 2060.
