## The Mission in the Game

“Fracture” is a multiplayer map from the *Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare* Reckoning DLC, so it has no campaign mission narrative, playable campaign character, mission date, briefing, route, or historical objective. Activision describes it as an **arctic excavation site** on the shelf of a glacier. The combat space is built around heavy equipment, wind turbines and exposed glacier terrain, with a timed environmental event in which part of the ice sheet breaks away and one of the map's lanes sinks.

The Call of Duty Wiki likewise places the map in the **Arctic Circle** and describes its terrain as an ice floe. It also notes a distant space elevator similar to the one on “Ascend,” but that visual link does not establish that Fracture shares Ascend's Gulf of Mexico location or its campaign relationship. No source reviewed documents Fracture as reusing or adapting a specific campaign mission.

That distinction matters for the closest campaign comparison. “Crash” (`aw-crash`) is also a glacier-set *Advanced Warfare* level, but it is explicitly located at Darwin Glacier in **Antarctica**. “Horizon” is explicitly documented as containing elements from “Crash”; Fracture is not. Fracture therefore should not inherit Darwin Glacier merely because both levels use frozen terrain.
