## The Mission in the Game

*Inside* is the second *Call of Duty: Black Ops 7* campaign mission, following *Exposure* and leading directly into *Distortion*. On June 18, 2035, David “Section” Mason, Mike Harper, Eric Samuels, and Leilani “50/50” Tupuola of JSOC Specter One operate in Avalon’s Eastern Sector after the Guild toxin incident in the preceding mission. After delivering the recovered Guild data drive to Karma, the team follows her to a grotto beneath Cliff Town. Specter One fights through the town, then searches the Water Treatment Plant for Guild biometric chips that can identify a missing Guild insider. After obtaining mobility equipment and using a Guild VTOL to improve the signal, the team assaults a shipyard/dock area and finds the insider in a shipping container. The mission ends as Mason begins hearing Frank Woods and the team is drawn into the hallucination that continues in *Distortion*.

The eastern-Avalon geography is unusually well defined for a fictional setting. Activision’s official Avalon tour places Cliff Town and Water Treatment together in the east/southeast, with Water Treatment downhill from Cliff Town and connected to it by an aqueduct. The related curated multiplayer records are *Cliff Town* (`bo7-cliff-town`), *Mission: Peak* (`bo7-mission-peak`), and *Mission: Trident* (`bo7-mission-trident`).

## The Real Place & Differences

The overall match is a **composite or fictionalized setting with a documented real-world inspiration for one major subarea**. Avalon itself is a fictional Mediterranean principality/city; there is no real Avalon Eastern Sector or Guild installation to geolocate. The stronger real-world lead comes from Cliff Town. Activision’s official *Black Ops 7* map guide explicitly states that Cliff Town is the *Black Ops II* 6v6 map **Yemen** reimagined inside Avalon. The original *Yemen* map is identified as being on Socotra Island, Yemen, and its internal map codename was `mp_socotra`; *Cliff Town* retains that lineage in the codename `mp_sat_rm_socotra2`.

Socotra is a real Yemeni island in the northwest Indian Ocean near the Gulf of Aden. UNESCO’s World Heritage documentation provides island-level geographic data for the Socotra property. This does **not** make Socotra the canonical location of Avalon: Socotra is outside the Mediterranean, while official *Black Ops 7* material consistently places Avalon on the Mediterranean coast. The Water Treatment Plant and the later shipyard/docks also have no independently documented Socotra counterpart. Socotra is therefore best treated as a real-world analogue inherited through the Cliff Town/Yemen layout, not as a literal location for the entire mission.

## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no matching real-world operation. *Inside* is a fictional JSOC mission dated 2035 involving the fictional Guild, its biometric security network, autonomous military technology, and a weaponized hallucination toxin. The earlier *Black Ops II* missions and multiplayer fiction associated with Socotra are also fictional events and must not be confused with historical operations.

The real-world connection is geographic and design lineage only: a substantial section of *Inside* uses Cliff Town, which is an Avalon reinterpretation of a map previously set on Socotra. No evidence was found for a real JSOC action on Socotra matching Specter One’s 2035 objectives, route, forces, or outcome.
