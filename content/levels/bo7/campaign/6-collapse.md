## The Mission in the Game

“Collapse” is the sixth mission of *Call of Duty: Black Ops 7*. It takes place on June 19, 2035, in Avalon's Northeast Sector and follows “Disruption.” The playable members of JSOC team Specter One are David “Section” Mason, Mike Harper, Eric Samuels, and Leilani “50/50” Tupuola.

After the team returns from Japan, its VTOL is attacked over Avalon by Guild drones. Because the ambush suggests that JSOC communications may have been compromised, Specter One pursues the surviving drone in hopes of recovering its flight data. The route leads to the Colosseum, where the squad clears the ruins, moves through underground spaces including the arena complex, and retrieves a network tablet so Karma can alter the drone's route. The team then returns to the surface, captures a Guild surface-to-air missile launcher, and defends it long enough to fire on the drone.

The mission ends when Samuels experiences a Cradle-induced hallucination of the 2025 Battle of Los Angeles, directly transitioning into “Fracture.” The Colosseum is also useful for locating the mission within Avalon's fictional geography: Activision's later tactical tour places the Colosseum, Excavation Site, Winery, and Resorts together in Zone 2, the northeast of Avalon. That helps explain why “Collapse” and the later mission “Quarantine” both use the Northeast Sector, but it does not establish a real-world site for either mission.
