## The Mission in the Game

*Fracture* is the seventh *Black Ops 7* campaign mission. David "Section" Mason, Mike Harper, Eric Samuels, and Leilani "50/50" Tupuola of JSOC Specter One remain caught in a shared Cradle hallucination that begins at the end of *Collapse* (`bo7-collapse`). In the real-world campaign chronology they had been pursuing a damaged Guild drone through Avalon's Northeast Sector on June 19, 2035; *Quarantine* (`bo7-quarantine`) resumes with the team recovering in Avalon after the hallucinations.

The first playable vision is labeled Los Angeles, California, on June 19, 2025. Specter One follows the damaged drone through a violently distorted freeway landscape based on the earlier Battle of Los Angeles: road decks spiral and overlap, wrecked vehicles form climbing routes, hallucinated traffic crosses the path, and the sequence recalls Anderson's FA38, the mobile SAM turret, President Marion Bosworth, and Samuels' role in her protection detail. The team eventually reaches the crashed drone, extracts its data, and tries to transmit it to Karma.

The hallucination then shifts to 50/50's 2034 memory of "Roji, Japan"; the mission's location listing identifies this sequence with Kōchi, Japan. It represents her failed attempt to intervene in an ongoing massacre before support arrived. The final vision moves to the fictional USS *Barack Obama* on June 18, 2025, where Harper's memory of Javier Salazar's betrayal becomes a boss fight against the gigantic "Nightmare Harper" on the carrier's flight deck.

## The Real Place & Differences

The level is best classified overall as a **composite or fictionalized setting**: Los Angeles and Kōchi are real places, but the playable spaces are explicitly hallucinations, while the USS *Barack Obama* is a fictional and mobile carrier rather than a fixed terrestrial site.

The Los Angeles segment can be narrowed substantially beyond the previous city-center marker. *Fracture* explicitly places the team on the freeway destruction from the 2025 Battle of Los Angeles and recreates specific details from *Black Ops II*'s *Cordis Die*, including Anderson's FA38 and the SAM turret used by Section. *Cordis Die* begins with Mason's convoy on Highway 110. This makes the real Route 110/Harbor Freeway corridor through Downtown Los Angeles the strongest geographic anchor for the hallucination. Caltrans identifies Route 110's Harbor Freeway through Los Angeles, and City of Los Angeles planning material documents Harbor Freeway access at 8th and 9th Streets. The correspondence is geographic, not architectural: *Fracture* twists the freeway into impossible spirals, suspended road decks, portals, and moving traffic hazards, so no individual interchange can defensibly be treated as an exact reconstruction.

Kōchi is a confirmed real city designation in the mission record, but the playable dialogue calls the remembered site "Roji, Japan." No independently verified real Kōchi landmark or district matching that in-game site was found in the reviewed authoritative place sources. The existing Kōchi city marker is therefore more defensible than inventing a specific massacre site or building.

## The Real Mission & Differences

No matching real-world military operation is documented for any of *Fracture*'s three sequences. The 2025 drone attack on Los Angeles and the presidential evacuation belong to the fictional *Black Ops II* timeline; *Fracture* is revisiting those in-universe events rather than depicting a historical battle. Route 110 is real, but the combat, drone attack, SAM engagement, characters, and mission objectives are fictional.

The 2034 "Roji" massacre in Kōchi is likewise an in-universe future event, not a documented historical operation, and the USS *Barack Obama* sequence revisits a fictional carrier and betrayal from the franchise. The mission therefore should not be used as evidence for real combat at either Los Angeles or Kōchi.
