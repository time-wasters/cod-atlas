## The Mission in the Game

“Quarantine” is the eighth *Call of Duty: Black Ops 7* campaign mission. On June 19, 2035, David “Section” Mason, Mike Harper, Eric Samuels, and Leilani “50/50” Tupuola of the fictional JSOC team Specter One operate in Avalon's Northeast Sector. It follows “Fracture” (`bo7-fracture`) and precedes “Suppression” (`bo7-suppression`).

After the hallucination-heavy events of “Fracture,” Specter One returns to Avalon's beachline and learns that Slade “Razor” Barrick has sabotaged the recovered Guild data. Karma salvages information identifying bioengineer Gideon Falkner and coordinates for his base of operations: a penthouse in a luxury resort. The team fights through the resort, reaches the penthouse, discovers a concealed laboratory, and is attacked through C-Link disruption and Cradle-induced hallucinations. After overcoming the disruption, the team captures Falkner and extracts information about the Sanctum before continuing there.

The resort is a substantially stronger geographic clue than the generic “Northeast Sector” label. Activision's later official Avalon tactical tour places a **Resorts** point of interest in **Zone 2: Northeast** and describes the Avalon Resort as a huge waterfront building whose elevator shaft leads to a penthouse based on the *Black Ops 6* multiplayer map “Skyline.” The official “Skyline” guide likewise describes a luxury resort rooftop and penthouse. The documented *Black Ops 7* multiplayer map “Mission: Edge” is also tied to “Quarantine” by the Call of Duty Wiki and depicts Avalon's dense hotel district, so the mission is best understood as using a defined resort/downtown part of the fictional Avalon map rather than an unspecified point somewhere in the Mediterranean.

That geopolitical and coastal pattern is unusually close to Avalon's fictional setup.
