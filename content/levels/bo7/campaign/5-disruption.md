## The Mission in the Game

“Disruption” is the fifth campaign mission in *Call of Duty: Black Ops 7*. It takes place on June 19, 2035, with JSOC Specter Two — Slade “Razor” Barrick, Nora Anderson, Wei Lin, and Axel Vermaak — operating in Japan. The mission itself labels the opening location **“Japan, Tokyo Bay.”**

The preceding mission, “Escalation” (`bo7-escalation`), establishes why Specter Two is sent there: Karma needs another quantum computer to decrypt the Guild data drive, and Specter One identifies the surviving machine inside a Guild facility in Tokyo called **the Forge**. “Disruption” begins with Specter Two approaching a Guild-linked company yacht in Tokyo Bay to capture executive Toru Fukasawa and use him to bypass the Forge’s security. Fukasawa is found dead, so the team abandons the covert plan and rams the yacht into the harbor-side facility.

The playable route then moves through the Forge, its security systems and lower quantum-computer level. Specter Two decrypts the key, escapes through a tunnel and subway station, emerges into the Tokyo streets, and traverses elevated urban structures toward the fictional **Kawauso Tower**, where the team fights a Guild VTOL before exfiltration. The campaign explicitly reuses or connects this mission with the multiplayer spaces **Hijacked**, **The Forge**, and **Toshin**: the first is the yacht, the second is the Guild research complex, and the third corresponds to the later Tokyo urban environment.

The following mission, “Collapse” (`bo7-collapse`), returns to Specter One in Avalon and opens after Razor reports his team’s success in Japan and the delivery of the key to Karma. This chronology is important for the atlas marker: “Disruption” is a geographically separate Tokyo operation between two Avalon missions, not an extension of the Avalon combat area.
