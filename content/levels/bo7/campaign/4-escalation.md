## The Mission in the Game

*Escalation* is the fourth *Call of Duty: Black Ops 7* campaign mission. It takes place on June 18, 2035 in Avalon's Central Sector and follows JSOC's Specter One team: David "Section" Mason, Mike Harper, Eric Samuels, and Leilani "50/50" Tupuola. It begins immediately after the hallucination-heavy events of *Distortion*. The wounded Guild insider identifies the bioweapon as the Cradle, while the team learns that the apparent Raul Menendez threat was a deepfake connected to Guild CEO Emma Kagan.

Karma cannot decrypt the Guild data drive without access to another quantum computer, so Specter One searches for Guild network credentials. The team investigates a van and is ambushed by Guild sniper Priya Zaveri, pursues her through the sector, kills her, and takes her access card. Karma then directs the squad to a Guild data bunker, where they fight through the defenses and spike several servers until they obtain infrastructure-mapping data. The recovered information reveals another quantum computer at the Forge in Tokyo, directly establishing the objective for the following mission, *Disruption*.

A detailed walkthrough identifies the playable route as beginning around the Roman Canal and Aqueduct, then passing the Art Warehouse and Heliport before reaching the Communication Tower area and the Guild data bunker. Activision's later official Avalon tactical tour independently places the Aqueduct, Art Warehouse, Heliport, Communication Tower, and nearby Water Treatment facilities within the same connected eastern/southeastern landscape. This also fits the broader geography used by *Inside* (`bo7-inside`), which is set in Avalon's Eastern Sector and begins around Cliff Town. These relationships help locate *Escalation* within the fictional Avalon map, but they do not establish a real-world bunker or aqueduct.
