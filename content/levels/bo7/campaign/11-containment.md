## The Mission in the Game

“Containment” is the eleventh and final story mission of the *Call of Duty: Black Ops 7* campaign before the separate Endgame activity. It takes place on June 19, 2035 at Blackheart, an offshore Guild installation associated with Avalon. David “Section” Mason, Mike Harper, Eric Samuels, and Leilani “50/50” Tupuola operate as JSOC’s Specter One; all four are playable campaign characters.

The mission is a direct continuation of `bo7-breakpoint`. At the end of “Breakpoint,” Karma traces the Cradle shipments to a cluster of mining rigs called Blackheart. Marshall specifies that the rig Specter One needs is only a few miles off Avalon’s coast and tells the team to secure a boat. In “Containment,” the team infiltrates that facility, descends into the industrial complex below the ocean surface, discovers Cradle production and shipping infrastructure, disables the shipping systems, and turns a bioreactor into a destructive overload. After fighting Emma Kagan’s G-X Strider units, Specter One captures Kagan and escapes by boat while Blackheart is destroyed; the team then heads back toward Avalon.

Blackheart is also represented by the related multiplayer level `bo7-blackheart`. The campaign article explicitly lists Blackheart as the mission’s multiplayer map, while Activision’s multiplayer guide describes the same name as a nautical drilling platform with a central rotating drill, barge, diving bell, grinder pit, and seabed-mining machinery. That relationship helps identify the fictional installation and its industrial character, but it does not supply a real-world position.

Activision places the campaign in and around the Mediterranean city of Avalon and describes Avalon as a city designed from the ground up to serve the campaign’s narrative and gameplay. The Call of Duty Wiki further identifies Avalon as a fictional principality on the Mediterranean coast.

The most useful geographic evidence is therefore relational rather than absolute: Blackheart lies a few miles offshore from Avalon, is reachable from the Avalon docks by boat, and the survivors return to Avalon afterward. The mission’s industrial geometry is much more elaborate than an ordinary surface drilling platform because the playable route continues through submerged production, shipping, reactor, and combat spaces built around the fictional Cradle program.

## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no matching real-world mission or historical operation. The in-game raid occurs on June 19, 2035, a future date, against the fictional Guild and its fictional Cradle bioweapon program. No evidence was found that Blackheart represents a named real drilling platform, mining complex, military target, or documented JSOC operation.

The route from the Avalon docks in “Breakpoint” to Blackheart in “Containment,” the destruction of the bioreactor, the G-X Strider fight, Kagan’s capture, and the return toward Avalon are therefore campaign chronology rather than historical events. Likewise, the multiplayer map `bo7-blackheart` shares the fictional installation but has no historical objective that can independently fix the campaign marker.
