## The Mission in the Game

*Endgame* is the replayable final part of *Call of Duty: Black Ops 7*'s Co-Op Campaign rather than another conventional linear mission. The Call of Duty Wiki places the canonical continuation on June 20, 2035, after *Containment*, with JSOC Specter One operating in the Principality of Avalon; Mason, Harper, Samuels, and 50/50 are the canonical squad, while the wider Operator roster is a gameplay option rather than part of that canonical team.

Official Call of Duty material describes Endgame as beginning after the first eleven campaign missions. JSOC continues its incursions across Avalon, searching the Guild's remaining Command Centers while exposure zones leak a mysterious toxin into the city. Endgame opens the whole Avalon playspace: players drop in by wingsuit, move on foot or by vehicle, choose dynamic Activities and Assignments, increase Combat Rating, enter progressively more dangerous zones, and ideally exfil by VTOL to preserve progression. It is therefore both a narrative continuation and a repeatable open PvE mode, not a single fixed route through one district.

This relationship matters for the atlas marker. Earlier Avalon campaign missions are confined to individual fictional sectors or facilities, whereas Endgame exposes the city-state at map scale and therefore provides a much larger set of geographic and architectural clues.

## The Real Place & Differences

Avalon is fictional. Franchise lore describes it as the **Principality of Avalon**, a Mediterranean city-state that was formerly part of France. No real city named Avalon occupies the in-game position, so the canonical setting cannot receive an exact real-world marker.

The strongest real-world analogue found is **Monaco**, and the match is classified here as a **plausible inspiration/analogue**, not a confirmed real location. Official Avalon map material documents a dense Mediterranean coastal settlement with a lavish casino, marina and docks, historic town fabric, an old cathedral and monument, steep/high districts, a stadium, coastal fortifications, resorts, and an urban road race called the “Avalon 35.” Monaco is likewise a compact Mediterranean principality and city-state on the French Riviera, with Monte-Carlo's casino, Monaco Cathedral and the historic Rock, Port Hercule and other marinas, dense development climbing steep terrain, Stade Louis-II, and the Monaco Grand Prix street circuit.

That combination is substantially more specific than a generic point in the Mediterranean Sea or an unspecified southern-French coast. It also fits Avalon's political form: both are Mediterranean principalities closely tied geographically to France. However, no authoritative developer statement was found explicitly saying that Avalon **is based on Monaco**. Avalon also contains large fictional islands, waterways, industrial areas, military facilities, an excavation complex and a colosseum-like site that do not reproduce Monaco one-for-one. Its scale and road network are deliberately rearranged for gameplay. The Monaco identification must therefore remain an inference, not canon.

## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no documented real-world counterpart to Endgame's June 2035 JSOC operation. The Guild, its Command Centers, the toxin exposure zones, the hostile creatures and machines, the Combat Rating system, dynamic assignments, and the repeated extraction loop are fictional or gameplay constructs.

Monaco is used only as the closest supported geographic and urban analogue for Avalon. The marker does **not** imply that JSOC, a real U.S. special-operations organization, conducted this fictional operation in Monaco, nor that any real historical battle or mission corresponds to Endgame. The relevant real-world comparison is place morphology and landmark pattern, not military chronology.
