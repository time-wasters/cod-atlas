## The Mission in the Game

The atlas classifies **The Frozen Dawn** as `multiplayer`, but it is a cooperative Nazi Zombies story map rather than a conventional competitive multiplayer arena. It follows **The Tortured Path** and is set months after 18–19 April 1945. The main playable cast is Drostan Hynd, Olivia Durant, Marie Fischer, and Jefferson Potts. Official Call of Duty material opens the scenario with the group isolated in Antarctica amid the burning wreckage of an enemy airship, after which they descend into the fictional Lost City of Thule through frozen subterranean tunnels and chambers and ultimately confront the God-King.

The Wiki infobox calls the setting the “Lost City of Thule, New Swabia, Antarctica,” but the evidence inside this specific map is more complicated. One of Vivian Harris's collectible text files reports that Captain Saburov survived the crash at the **western end of the Ronne Ice Shelf**, with four sets of footprints leading inland. Another file says Harris had earlier left for New Swabia. The preceding map, **The Tortured Path**, explicitly uses the New Swabia briefing that is also cited by The Frozen Dawn's Wiki infobox. Taken together, the story places New Swabia in the preceding leg of the journey while tying The Frozen Dawn's crash site directly to the Ronne Ice Shelf.

## The Real Place & Differences

The **Ronne Ice Shelf** is a real, enormous ice shelf on the southern side of the Weddell Sea. The SCAR Composite Gazetteer describes it as extending along the western part of the Filchner–Ronne system and gives a reference position of 78°30′S, 61°00′W. That real geographic feature is the closest directly documented match to the playable map because the in-map recording names its western end.

The level is nevertheless a **composite or fictionalized setting**, not a reconstruction of a documented site. No real Lost City of Thule is known beneath the Ronne Ice Shelf. Classical Thule belonged to ancient northern geography: surviving traditions place it somewhere north of Britain or in the far North Atlantic, not Antarctica. The game's monumental underground city, basalt-like architecture, supernatural machinery, and extensive cavern network therefore cannot be matched to a real Antarctic archaeological site.

**New Swabia (Neuschwabenland)** is also a real Antarctic regional name, but it lies in Dronning Maud Land, far to the east of the Ronne Ice Shelf. The German Antarctic Expedition of 1938–39 surveyed a broad area there and named it Neuschwabenland. It remains relevant to the Zombies storyline and is a plausible secondary historical inspiration, but it is weaker than Ronne Ice Shelf for this level's primary atlas marker because the crew has already left that area when the Frozen Dawn crash is reported.

## The Real Mission & Differences

There is **no documented real 1945 military mission corresponding to The Frozen Dawn**. The MFAA/B.A.T. expedition, Geistkraft, undead forces, the God-King, Barbarossa's supernatural sword, and the Lost City of Thule are fictional.

The closest real German Antarctic context is the 1938–39 expedition to Dronning Maud Land. It conducted aerial surveying and mapping in the area subsequently called Neuschwabenland, but a peer-reviewed review of the supposed “Nazi Antarctic base” story concludes that there was no secret German wartime base in Dronning Maud Land. The game therefore blends a real pre-war German expedition and real Antarctic place names with later conspiracy mythology and supernatural fiction.

The game's chronology also contains Antarctic anachronisms. In recordings associated with **The Tortured Path**, the Bureau says discoveries during **Operation Highjump** led it to increased Nazi activity in New Swabia, even though the Zombies story is dated only months after April 1945. The real U.S. Navy Operation Highjump ran from December 1946 to April 1947. Likewise, the Ronne Ice Shelf area was aerially observed by the Ronne Antarctic Research Expedition in late 1947, so characters using “Ronne Ice Shelf” as an established place name in a 1945-set story is retrospective.
