mission narrative, playable character, assigned unit, canonical battle date or
scripted historical objective. The map pits the Red Army against the Wehrmacht.
The Call of Duty Wiki documents it as being based on the singleplayer level

## The Real Place & Differences

The strongest real-world match is **Ponyri railway station in Ponyri, Kursk
Oblast**. This is a confirmed place-level identification rather than a generic
Russia fallback: the multiplayer map is explicitly derived from the Ponyri
singleplayer level, that level attacks the railway station, and the multiplayer
map itself is described as a large Russian train-station battlefield. Ponyri
Station was also a genuine focal point of the July 1943 fighting on the northern
face of the Kursk salient.

The present railway site preserves the same geographic anchor, but not an
unchanged 1943 streetscape. The wartime station was on the front line and the
building was badly damaged; the current station building is a postwar
reconstruction dating from the late 1940s to early 1950s. The game likewise
compresses railway facilities, open fields, woodland, buildings and a tower
into a balanced combat space. Its exact distances, building shapes and vehicle

## The Real Mission & Differences

The closest historical context is the battle for Ponyri Station during
Operation Citadel, especially 7–10 July 1943. U.S. Army Combat Studies Institute
research identifies Major General Mikhail Enshin’s **307th Rifle Division** as
the principal Soviet formation defending the station sector, heavily reinforced
by artillery, engineers and armour. German forces from the 292nd Infantry
Division and other formations attacked toward the station with substantial
armoured support. On 7 July the station changed hands repeatedly; Soviet
counterattacks with the 129th Tank Brigade and later reinforcements continued
through the following days.

That history is context for the setting, not a multiplayer mission identity.
There is no documented real operation corresponding to a round of multiplayer,
and the Red Army/Wehrmacht team structure, respawns, objectives and available
vehicles are gameplay abstractions. The linked singleplayer mission is itself a
fictionalized composite and assigns its protagonist to the 67th Guards Rifle
Division, which was historically fighting on the southern face of the Kursk
salient rather than at Ponyri.
