## The Real Mission & Differences

Because this is a round-based multiplayer map, its U.S.-versus-Wehrmacht fighting does not represent a single documented historical mission. The closest supported historical context is the real fighting for Foy on **13 January 1945**, also adapted separately by the linked singleplayer level `cod-uo-foy`.

Official U.S. Army material records that the **506th Parachute Infantry Regiment, 101st Airborne Division** seized Foy on 13 January. Liberation Route Europe describes Easy Company attacking across the fields while elements of Item Company advanced toward the church crossroads; after the attack stalled, Ronald Speirs replaced Norman Dike, and the village was secured later that day. The multiplayer map’s repeatable tank battles, usable FlaK 88s, fixed team bases, respawns, and bridge fighting are therefore gameplay elements, not a historical sequence or a claim about exact unit positions.
