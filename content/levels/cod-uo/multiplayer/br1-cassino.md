## The Real Place & Differences

The strongest real-world match is **Cassino, Lazio**, the town below Monte Cassino and its abbey. This is a **confirmed real location at city/battlefield level**, but the playable layout is best treated as a fictionalized or composite urban space rather than an exact reconstruction. ## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no single real mission corresponding to the multiplayer match. Historically, Cassino was a key German defensive position on the Gustav Line controlling the approaches to the Liri Valley and the road toward Rome. U.S. Army historical material records the French Expeditionary Corps attacking north of Cassino in January 1944 and elements of the U.S. 34th Infantry Division entering Cassino itself in early February before the fighting continued under other Allied formations. The wider Fifth Army history describes repeated costly assaults around Cassino before the Allied advance resumed toward Rome.

The multiplayer factions therefore reflect real participants in the broader campaign, but not one shared historical action inside the map's exact combat space. The U.S.-versus-Germany pairing in *United Offensive* has a direct basis in American fighting around and inside Cassino. French forces also fought in the Cassino campaign, but the *Big Red One* France-versus-Germany pairing should not be read as evidence that this particular town layout reproduces a documented French assault. The Wiki's January-May date range compresses the several battles around Cassino into one multiplayer setting.
