## The Mission in the Game

“Sicily Chase” is the eighth mission in *Call of Duty: United Offensive*, the
final mission of its British campaign, and a direct continuation of “Sicily 1”
(`cod-uo-sicily-1`). The game retains the previous mission's stated date of 12
July 1943 and its fictional protagonists: Sergeant James Doyle and Major Gerald
Ingram of an SAS assault force at Capo Murro di Porco on the Sicilian coast.

The mission opens with Ingram driving Doyle away from the sabotaged coastal
battery on a motorcycle. German trucks and motorcycles pursue them along rural
roads and through a compact village. Ingram avoids a tank but crashes the
motorcycle, forcing the two men to continue through houses, courtyards and
alleys on foot. They descend beside a cliff, fight through a bunker and try to
reach a small dock where Private Higgs is waiting with a fishing boat.

Higgs and the boat are destroyed before they can leave. Doyle and Ingram seize
an armed German patrol boat instead, fight several enemy boats among the coastal
rocks and reach the open sea. The three guns sabotaged in “Sicily 1” explode
behind them, and Ingram says that *Ulster Monarch* is waiting offshore. The
level ends there; the ship is not reached on screen.

## The Real Place & Differences

The game remains at Capo Murro di Porco and the Maddalena Peninsula south of
Syracuse. This is the real area of the Lamba Doria coastal battery attacked by
the Special Raiding Squadron (SRS) during Operation Husky. The peninsula's
limestone cliffs, rocky shore, dry terrain, scattered farm buildings and
military positions provide a recognizable basis for the level's coastal
landscape.

Masseria Damerio, west of the battery, is the closest documented historical
anchor for the action after Lamba Doria fell. It was not an incidental modern
place name: Allied planning identified the farmhouse as a fortified position,
and No. 3 Troop was assigned to seize it and cut the only road from the west so
that reinforcements could not reach the battery. The raiders subsequently moved
toward the masseria to regroup before continuing across the peninsula. The
place is still named in provincial planning maps and in a georeferenced list of
Syracuse's cultural properties.

The match is operational rather than visual. No evidence was found that the
game's tunnel, densely built village, motorcycle route, crash barrier, bunker
passage or small defended harbour reproduces Masseria Damerio or another
identifiable route on the peninsula. The actual cape was a rural military
landscape organized around farmhouses, local roads and dispersed Italian
batteries. Its southern and eastern sides are predominantly high rocky cliffs;
the real raiders used landing craft and climbed ashore where the western cliffs
were lower. The game's continuous road-to-village-to-beach route is best
treated as a compressed, fictional layout.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The historical SRS action did continue after the destruction of Lamba Doria,
but not as an escape. On the night of 9-10 July 1943, the force commanded by
Major Robert Blair “Paddy” Mayne landed from *Ulster Monarch*, scaled the cape
and captured the Italian battery. British sappers disabled its three 152 mm
guns. By about 05:30 the raiders had moved clear and were heading toward
Masseria Damerio when another battery opened fire on Allied shipping.

Mayne then extended the operation across the Maddalena Peninsula. The SRS
attacked battery AS 493, used Casa Mallia during that advance and went on to
silence the Emanuele Russo and AS 309 positions nearer Syracuse harbour. The
raiders spent the night at a local farm, moved west on 11 July to meet the
British 5th Infantry Division, handed over their prisoners and entered
Syracuse. They returned to *Ulster Monarch* before their next operation, the
12 July amphibious assault on Augusta.

The game therefore combines real names and adjacent events but changes their
meaning:

- The real action after Lamba Doria was an organized advance against further
  positions, not a two-man flight back to the sea.
- The real battery raid took place on 10 July. The game's 12 July date instead
  coincides with the later SRS landing at Augusta, which occurred well north of
  Capo Murro di Porco.
- *Ulster Monarch* genuinely carried the SRS and later landed it at Augusta,
  but the sources do not describe the ship waiting to extract two men after a
  patrol-boat battle.
- The documented defenders on the Maddalena Peninsula were principally Italian
  coastal troops and MILMART artillerymen. No historical source found for this
  analysis matches the level's German road pursuit, tank encounter or flotilla
  of armed boats.
- Doyle, Ingram and Higgs are fictional. The motorcycle chase, village battle,
  Higgs's death, theft of a German boat and running naval gunfight are dramatic
  inventions rather than a reconstruction of an SRS action.

“Sicily Chase” is consequently a fictional escape appended to a genuine raid.
Its closest historical counterpart is the SRS movement from Lamba Doria toward
Masseria Damerio and the other peninsula batteries, but the real force moved
inland and continued fighting rather than fleeing by sea.
