## The Mission in the Game

“Kharkov Outskirts” is the twelfth campaign mission in *Call of Duty: United
Offensive*. The player controls the fictional Private Yuri Petrenko, who has
left the 5th Guards Tank Army and rejoined his infantry comrades in the 15th
Guards Rifle Division. His introductory letter places the unit on the edge of
Kharkov on 22 August 1943. The playable action begins at 12:35.

The briefing says the force must advance quickly and capture the central

## The Real Place & Differences

Kharkov is the Russian form of **Kharkiv**, now in Ukraine. The city was a
major industrial and railway centre, and rail facilities were meaningful
military objectives. The 15th Guards Rifle Division's documented approach came
from the east and southeast. One published reconstruction records the division
reaching the eastern city edge at 04:00 on 23 August, taking an experimental
agricultural station at 08:00 and **Osnova Railway Station** at 09:00 before
entering farther into Kharkiv.

Osnova is a large railway junction in southern Kharkiv, not the main passenger
station west of the city centre. It therefore provides a real landmark on the
historical division's route, but it does not prove that the game's ruins,
square or sequence of artillery positions represent Osnova. No evidence was
found that the level reproduces a particular neighbourhood or surviving
building.

The game also blurs “outskirts” and inner-city fighting. Petrenko begins among
dense multi-storey ruins and quickly reaches a square, while the documented
15th Guards route first crossed the eastern edge and continued toward Osnova.
Kharkiv was heavily damaged in the war and has changed extensively since 1943,
so visual resemblance alone cannot fix the level to a present street plan.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The historical fighting formed the closing phase of the
**Belgorod-Kharkiv offensive**, also known as Operation Polkovodets Rumyantsev.
The Steppe Front's 53rd and 69th Armies approached Kharkiv from the west and
north, while the 7th Guards Army advanced into the eastern side. The **15th
Guards Rifle Division was a real formation in the 7th Guards Army**, and it was
one of the divisions later awarded the “Kharkov” honorific. The game therefore
uses a historically relevant unit rather than an invented divisional label.

The chronology is substantially compressed and moved forward. The game has
the 15th Guards fighting well inside the urban area at 12:35 on **22 August**.
Historical accounts instead place the decisive entry during the night of
22–23 August and the division itself only at the eastern edge at 04:00 on
**23 August**. Kharkiv Historical Museum's summary describes the 7th Guards
Army entering from the east at dawn and the city being cleared around midday
on the 23rd. Another museum study cautions that most German troops had already
withdrawn before the celebrated night assault, leaving covering forces and a
more complicated final clearance than the traditional liberation narrative
suggests.

Artillery-supported infantry and armour fighting against German rearguards is
plausible in this broader operation. Petrenko's three precisely scripted fire
missions, the Tiger encounter, the Panzerfaust kills and the squad's path
through the square are not documented incidents. No evidence was found that
the 15th Guards had the specific historical task of seizing Kharkiv's central
passenger station. The division's documented railway objective was Osnova;
the game appears to combine the real eastern advance with a fictional direct
push toward the central station.

The mission also presents the battle as a continuous daytime assault against
strong occupied blocks. That is effective game pacing, but it shifts the real
unit's entry by roughly a day, compresses a multi-army encirclement and
withdrawal into one squad's route, and gives fictional characters decisive
actions that cannot be tied to the historical record.
