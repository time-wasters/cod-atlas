The [CoD Wiki article](https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/Blowtorch_%26_Corkscrew)
identifies the mission setting as Wana Ridge on Okinawa. The U.S. Marine Corps
[Okinawa battlefield staff ride](https://www.usmcu.edu/Portals/218/Okinawa%20Staff%20Ride%20Pub%20Proof%205%20for%20Web.pdf)
places Wana Ridge and Wana Draw within modern Sueyoshi Park, whose address is
also listed by the [City of Naha](https://www.city.naha.okinawa.jp/shisetsu/sports/1007498/1004874.html).
The marker uses the [mapped park center](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/54500385)
as a representative point and should not be read as the exact position of the
mission's fictionalized battlefield.
