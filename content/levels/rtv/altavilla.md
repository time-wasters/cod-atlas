## The Mission in the Game

The mission takes place near Altavilla on 18 September 1943. The player is
Private Derrick Warren of the U.S. 82nd Airborne Division, advancing uphill
with Captain Peterson's men to recapture the town and prevent the Germans from
driving the Salerno beachhead back into the sea. The route passes trenches, a
machine-gun nest and three artillery positions before entering the town. From
high ground, Warren marks German tanks for American artillery.

The playable soldiers and this particular chain of objectives have not been
identified as a documented historical patrol. They appear to dramatize the
82nd Airborne's genuine role in the battle for Altavilla and Hill 424.

## The Real Place & Differences

Altavilla Silentina stands on high ground east of the Allied landing beaches
at Paestum. The most important nearby terrain was **Hill 424**, locally known
as **Quota 424**, whose summit overlooks the town and the Sele plain. Its view
made it valuable to German forces trying to protect their withdrawal and to
the Allies expanding the Salerno beachhead. Fighting moved across the slopes
and through Altavilla's historic centre, so the level's combination of an
uphill approach, town fighting and a final observation position has a strong
geographical basis.

The game compresses and rearranges the landscape into a linear combat route.
No evidence was found that its trenches, gun sites, buildings or tank
positions reproduce particular surviving sites around Altavilla.

## The Real Mission & Differences

Operation Avalanche began with the Allied landings at Salerno on 9 September
1943. The U.S. 36th Infantry Division fought repeatedly for Altavilla and Hill
424 during the following week. The 504th Parachute Infantry Regiment of the
82nd Airborne had parachuted into the Paestum beachhead during the emergency,
rather than directly onto Altavilla, and its 1st and 2nd Battalions then moved
overland toward the hills. They attacked the Altavilla hill mass during the
night of 16–17 September and endured heavy German artillery fire on the 17th.

The level's date is significant but its action is heightened for gameplay. The
U.S. Army's campaign history reports that German forces evacuated Altavilla
and Hill 424 as part of their wider withdrawal; by late afternoon on 18
September, tanks of the 191st Tank Battalion accompanied the paratroopers into
a deserted town. Thus the real 504th PIR connection, place, high-ground
objective and 18 September occupation all fit, while the mission shifts the
most intense fighting onto the final day and invents a continuous assault
through occupied gun and tank positions.

## Marker Position Explanation

The marker at `40.532, 15.1406` is the documented roadside memorial on Hill
424 at Località Piano delle Rose. American War Memorials Overseas identifies
the point as the wartime Hill 424 site and records its coordinates; the local
Pro Loco describes a memorial near the 424-metre summit dedicated to those who
fell in the fighting. This is more specific and historically relevant than the
previous town-centre marker, although it should not be interpreted as the
starting point of the game's fictional route or the position of every real
engagement.

The stored Google Maps URL is an API search for the named Hill 424 memorial at
Altavilla Silentina. The atlas marker itself uses the independently curated
coordinates above.
