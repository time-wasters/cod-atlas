## The Challenge in the Game

The Firing Range is a selectable Create-A-Class practice feature, classified
as Other / Challenge. It has no numbered challenge or campaign objective.
Players test weapons against practice dummies in five lanes for shotguns,
submachine guns, assault rifles, light machine guns and sniper rifles.
The Wiki identifies its setting as the SAS training compound in Credenhill,
United Kingdom, and its campaign source as F.N.G. (`cod4-f-n-g`).
