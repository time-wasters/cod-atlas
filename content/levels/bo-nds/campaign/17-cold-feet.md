## The Real Mission & Differences

**No matching real mission was established.** No historical evidence examined connects Shaw's fictional team, a Nova-6 shipment, and the destruction of an Arctic submarine facility in 1968. A unit deployment, vessel loss, or battle site cannot therefore anchor this marker.

The similarly named **Operation COLDFEET** is a useful comparison to rule out. CIA's historical study describes a 1962 intelligence operation against abandoned Soviet drifting station **NP 8**, involving U.S. Air Force Major James Smith and U.S. Navy Reserve Lieutenant Leonard LeSchack. They investigated the station and were recovered using a B-17 equipped with the Fulton system. The intelligence included Soviet work relevant to detecting submarines beneath the ice. This was an investigation of an abandoned station, not the game's armed submarine-demolition raid. The shared name does not establish developer inspiration or justify using NP 8's drifting position. [CIA, Leary]

The historical operation belongs to May–June 1962, whereas the game's concluding narrative belongs to 1968. The CIA study itself notes differences among accounts; its reconstructed extraction chronology should not be used to manufacture an exact date for the unrelated game mission.
