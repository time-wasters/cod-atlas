## The Mission in the Game

Fortune's Keep is a multiplayer Battle Royale/Resurgence map, not a campaign mission, so it has no fixed playable character, unit, briefing, route, or historical objective. The original *Call of Duty: Warzone* version released in June 2022 and depicts a fictional island in the Adriatic Sea. Its Resurgence infil identifies the setting as **Operation: Dragons Cove** on **September 21, 1976**; Golden Plunder instead uses a June 2022 date, so these chyrons are playlist-specific framing rather than a single historical chronology.

High Moon Studios described Fortune's Keep as an original map inspired by multiple real Mediterranean locations. Its principal areas deliberately combine recognizable regional motifs: a dense seaside Town with cobblestone lanes and an old fortress, Smuggler's Cove and a cave-filled Grotto, a large fortified Keep, and a Winery with cellars and a nearby military camp. The fiction adds a history of pirates, smugglers, hidden treasure, mercenaries, and gold extraction. No documented relationship to a singleplayer campaign level was found in the reviewed official or Wiki material.

## The Real Place & Differences

**Classification: composite or fictionalized setting, with Vis, Croatia selected as the closest documented real-world analogue found.** Activision's own developer material rules out treating Fortune's Keep as a disguised one-to-one recreation: High Moon explicitly says the map was created as an original island using inspiration from real locations across the Mediterranean.

Vis is nevertheless a notably strong analogue for the combined clues. It is a Croatian island in the Adriatic with limestone and karst caves, fortified coastal sites, old settlements with colourful buildings and narrow cobblestone streets, vineyards, and a long fishing tradition. Around Vis town, **Fort George (George the Third Fortress)** guards the entrance to Vis Bay; nearby Rogačić Bay contains a military-built tunnel formerly used to shelter torpedo boats. Local heritage sources also document Vis as a centre of piracy, smuggling, and intelligence during the early nineteenth century. These features parallel several of Fortune's Keep's themes without requiring a literal architectural match.

The differences are substantial. Fort George was begun in 1812 and is therefore not the medieval Keep shown in the game. Vis is much larger and its towns, caves, vineyards, forts, and military works are dispersed rather than compressed into the arena-like layout of Fortune's Keep. Hvar is a credible alternative for the map's fortress-and-winery imagery because Hvar Town has the prominent Fortica fortress and the island has a major wine tradition, but Vis is the stronger overall anchor because it additionally matches the Adriatic setting, cave/tunnel geography, smuggling history, and the unusually relevant 1976 military context. No evidence was found that High Moon Studios specifically named Vis, Fort George, or Hvar as an inspiration.

## The Real Mission & Differences

No matching real military operation on September 21, 1976 is documented for Fortune's Keep, because Fortune's Keep itself is fictional. The closest useful historical context is that **Vis was then part of socialist Yugoslavia and was an active military zone**. The Local Museum of the City of Vis states that after the Second World War the island's strategic position led to dozens of military facilities, bases, tunnels, and underground shelters, and that it remained closed to foreigners until 1989. The Vis Tourist Board likewise records that Fort George continued in military use after the Second World War under the Yugoslav People's Army.

That context makes Vis unusually compatible with a fictional 1976 fortified Adriatic island, but it does not turn the game's story into history. The documented piracy and smuggling associations belong mainly to the Napoleonic period, while the game's mercenaries, treasure caches, gold extraction, and combat are fictional. No evidence was found for a battle, raid, or operation at Fort George or elsewhere on Vis corresponding to Operation: Dragons Cove on the game's stated date.
