## The Mission in the Game

*The Sins of the Father* is a singleplayer mission played as Sgt. John “Soap” MacTavish of the 22nd SAS Regiment on Day 5 at 06:34:26. The in-game title card gives the setting only as **Southern Russia**. Captain Price’s SAS team is operating jointly with SSgt. Griggs of the U.S. Marines and Russian Loyalists led by Sgt. Kamarov. Their objective is to capture Victor Zakhaev, son of Imran Zakhaev, so that he can be interrogated for his father’s location.

Kamarov leads the force toward an Ultranationalist vehicle checkpoint. The team eliminates the checkpoint guards, secures the position and uses the captured position and Russian clothing to prepare an ambush. When Victor Zakhaev’s convoy arrives, the ambush goes wrong and Victor escapes. Soap, Price, Gaz and Griggs pursue him through a built-up settlement, including yards, alleys and multi-storey structures, until he is cornered on a rooftop. Rather than be captured, Victor shoots himself.

The mission immediately follows *Heat* (`cod4-heat`), whose action ends with the SAS being extracted from Azerbaijan, and it precedes *Ultimatum* (`cod4-ultimatum`) in the Altai Mountains on Day 6. The transition therefore moves the joint force from the Caucasus/Azerbaijan theatre into southern Russia before the campaign makes a much larger jump to the Altai for Act III.

## The Real Place & Differences

The Call of Duty Wiki’s structured location field and Call of Duty Maps both identify the mission more specifically as **Uzlovoy, Krasnodar Krai, Russia**. Uzlovoy is a real settlement in Mostovsky District and forms part of the Bagovskoye rural settlement. Russian geographic sources place it at the confluence of the Bugunzh and Khodz rivers, south-west of Mostovskoy, at the northern foothills of the Caucasus. Regional planning documents also name the road from Mostovskoy through Bagovskaya to Uzlovoy, independently confirming the settlement and its road connection.

Modern descriptions portray Uzlovoy as a very small rural settlement rather than the sizeable combat space shown in the game. It has only a handful of streets, low-rise housing, a school and shops; local descriptions note that the paved approach road gives way to gravel streets inside the settlement. That is compatible with the mission’s broad southern-Russian/Caucasus-foothill atmosphere, but not with treating the game’s checkpoint, junkyard, commercial buildings, dense alleys or multi-storey final building as surveyed reproductions of real Uzlovoy.
