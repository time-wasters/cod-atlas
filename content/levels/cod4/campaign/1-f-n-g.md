## The Mission in the Game

“F.N.G.” is the opening tutorial mission of *Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare*. The player controls Sgt. John “Soap” MacTavish of the **22nd SAS Regiment** at an **SAS training compound in Credenhill, United Kingdom**. The current Call of Duty Wiki record dates the mission to **Day 0, 2011 at 06:30:12**; however, the Call of Duty Maps campaign overview labels it **Day 1, 2011**. Because the location, sequence and relationship to the next mission are consistent across the sources, this research leaves that day-number discrepancy explicit rather than silently choosing one version.

Soap begins with Gaz on a firing range, where the tutorial covers aiming, firing from the hip, shooting through cover, weapon switching and melee. He then meets Captain Price and runs a timed close-quarters course through a **cargo-ship bridge mock-up**, using an MP5 and flashbangs. The level ends with Price evaluating Soap's run.

The connection to the next campaign level, **“Crew Expendable” (`cod4-crew-expendable`)**, is direct rather than inferred: the Wiki describes the CQB course as a mock-up based on the cargo ship that Price's team will infiltrate in the following mission. This makes “F.N.G.” a preparation/training scene at the SAS home base, not part of the Bering Strait operation itself.

## The Real Place & Differences

The game names **Credenhill**, and that is a substantially more defensible real-world location than the existing United Kingdom fallback. Credenhill is a village northwest of Hereford. In 1996, a Ministry of Defence answer in Parliament stated that **22 SAS was to move from its then-current Stirling Lines site in Hereford to the former RAF site at Credenhill** because the old base was too small. Hereford City Council's historical summary records that the regiment moved to the RAF base at Credenhill in **2000**. The local parish council likewise identifies the former RAF site as the place into which the SAS moved.

By the game's 2011 setting, therefore, placing a 22 SAS training compound at Credenhill aligns with the real regiment's established base location. This is best classified as a **confirmed real location at the base/site level**, rather than merely a generic British analogue or country fallback.

The match should not be overstated. No evidence found establishes that the game's firing range, outdoor layout, obstacle elements or cargo-ship CQB mock-up reproduce specific buildings or training structures at Stirling Lines. The National Army Museum confirms that the SAS is associated with close-combat fighting and hostage rescue, so the broad CQB-training concept is credible, but the level's exact architecture and tutorial sequence remain a fictionalized game depiction.

## The Real Mission & Differences

There is **no matching historical combat mission** for “F.N.G.”. It is a fictional training/tutorial episode introducing Soap, Gaz and Captain Price and setting up the subsequent “Crew Expendable” raid. The real-world historical point relevant to this level is the location and use of Credenhill as the 22 SAS home base, not a documented operation on the morning represented in the game.

Public British Army material also cautions against treating the game's “fresh out of Selection” sequence as a literal representation of the real process. Modern UK Special Forces material describes a formal briefing and selection pathway, while Army responses note that significant details of Special Forces training are not discussed openly. Those modern sources cannot be projected unchanged back onto 2011, but they reinforce that the short weapons lesson and timed cargo-ship run in “F.N.G.” should be read as a compressed tutorial device rather than a documentary reconstruction of SAS induction.

Likewise, the transition from the Credenhill drill to “Crew Expendable” is part of the game's fictional campaign chronology. No evidence was found for a real 22 SAS raid from Credenhill matching the game's Bering Strait cargo-ship mission.
