## The Mission in the Game

"One Shot, One Kill" is a singleplayer flashback mission set in Pripyat, Ukraine, in winter 1996, three days after "All Ghillied Up" (`cod4-all-ghillied-up`). The player is Lieutenant Price, operating with Captain MacMillan. The mission begins on the upper floor of the Polissya Hotel, where the pair have waited for Imran Zakhaev to appear. Price uses an M82 .50-caliber rifle to attempt the long-range assassination while MacMillan calls the shot and warns about wind and other ballistic factors.

Zakhaev survives after losing an arm, and the mission turns into an escape through Pripyat. Price and MacMillan leave the hotel, fight through Ultranationalist forces, and evade a helicopter. After the helicopter crashes, MacMillan is injured and Price must carry him toward the extraction point. The final defense takes place at the Pripyat amusement park, with the Ferris wheel as the mission's most recognizable late-stage landmark, before the pair are evacuated by helicopter.

The mission is therefore a direct narrative continuation of "All Ghillied Up": the earlier level is the infiltration toward Pripyat and the assassination position, while "One Shot, One Kill" begins at that position and follows the attempted assassination and withdrawal.
