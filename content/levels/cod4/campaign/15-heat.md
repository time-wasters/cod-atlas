## The Mission in the Game

"Heat" is a singleplayer mission played as Sgt. John "Soap" MacTavish of the
22nd SAS Regiment. The mission title card gives **Day 4, 09:40:28** and
**Northern Azerbaijan**, eight hours after Khaled Al-Asad's death. Although
"All Ghillied Up" and "One Shot, One Kill" intervene in the campaign's playable
order as flashbacks, "Heat" is the direct chronological continuation of
"Safehouse" (`cod4-safehouse`) and returns to the same village and combat space.

Captain Price expects an Ultranationalist counterattack while the SAS waits for
extraction. The team first defends the southern approach from the ridgeline,
using prepared charges around phase lines Alpha and Bravo. The fighting then
reverses the earlier "Safehouse" assault: the player falls back uphill through
the village, uses the tavern detonators to delay the attackers, reaches the
farm/safehouse area, takes a Javelin from the barn to destroy incoming tanks,
and finally fights back downhill toward the changed extraction point, where
U.S. Marines under Staff Sgt. Griggs assist the evacuation. The church tower,
tavern, farm buildings, steep approaches and surrounding water/river terrain
are therefore elements of the fictional playable village, not verified
real-world landmarks.

## The Real Place & Differences

The current Call of Duty Wiki and Call of Duty Maps campaign overview identify
the village more specifically as **Amsar (Əmsar), Quba, Azerbaijan**. Amsar is
a real village in Quba District in northern Azerbaijan: Azerbaijani official
administrative and local-government sources list Amsar as a village and
municipal/administrative center. A published geographical-name entry places it
at approximately `41°19′53″N, 48°32′20″E`. Afad Qurbanov's study of the Amsar
dialect describes the village as about 5 km south of Quba, around 610 m above
sea level, on the foothill plain with the Qaraçay to the south. That broad
foothill setting is compatible with the game's hilly northern-Azerbaijan
presentation.

The match is nevertheless **a composite or fictionalized setting anchored to a
plausible specific real settlement**, not a confirmed reconstruction of
Amsar. The mission itself displays only "Northern Azerbaijan"; it does not name
Amsar or Quba in the title card. The exact Amsar attribution therefore comes
from secondary game-reference metadata rather than an explicit place name in
the mission transcript. Real-world sources also do not verify the mission's
church, tavern, safehouse farm, ridgelines, phase lines or extraction route as
Amsar landmarks. Azerbaijani cultural/religious listings do document an Amsar
village mosque, but no evidence was found that the conspicuous in-game church
tower corresponds to a real building in Amsar.

## The Real Mission & Differences

No matching real SAS/USMC mission or battle in Amsar in 2011 was found. The

Contemporary reporting on Azerbaijan in 2011 instead describes the country's
