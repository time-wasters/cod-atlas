## The Mission in the Game

“Farewell to Friends” is the ninth singleplayer level of *Call of Duty 2: Big Red One*. The game places Cpl. Roland Roger with the 1st Infantry Division's fictionalized Fox Company at **Troina, Sicily, on August 5, 1943 at 10:40**. The squad approaches Troina riding on tanks with Mount Etna visible behind the town. A German FlaK 88 destroys a vehicle in the column, forcing the squad to dismount and continue on foot through bomb-damaged streets and buildings. The route includes an MG position, artillery fire, the death of Victor Denley at a doorway, and a final push toward a German-held church. Roger's principal objective is to flank the church and destroy the FlaK 88 emplacement there with an explosive charge.

The mission follows “Piano Lupo” in the Sicily chapter. The level's briefing artwork shows the campaign progression from Gela through Piano Lupo to **Troina**, reinforcing that Troina is the intended destination rather than a generic point on eastern Sicily. The following level, “The Great Crusade,” jumps forward to the Normandy landings in June 1944, so it does not provide a geographically adjacent continuation for this marker.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The historical Battle of Troina was a prolonged fight by the U.S. 1st Infantry Division, reinforced by elements of the 9th Infantry Division and other Allied forces, against the German 15th Panzer Grenadier Division and associated Italian forces. The most severe fighting occurred on the ridges, hills, and approaches surrounding Troina rather than as the single continuous street assault presented in the game.

On **August 5, 1943**, the date shown by the level, the 1st Division resumed its attacks around Troina. The 26th Infantry remained heavily engaged around Monte Basilio, the attached 39th Infantry was fighting north of town, and the 16th Infantry spent the day trying to advance against Hills 1034 and 1006 west of Troina without gaining ground. German forces began withdrawing from Troina late that evening along Highway 120 toward Cesarò. After dawn on **August 6**, 16th Infantry patrols entered Troina and encountered only sporadic rifle fire that was quickly suppressed.

The game's action therefore compresses and rearranges the historical chronology. Its tank-borne entry into Troina, sustained close-range street fighting on August 5, Denley's death, and the mission to capture and demolish a church FlaK 88 are not documented as the historical 1st Division action for that date. The real battle was extremely costly and destructive, but the final American entry into the town came after the main German withdrawal rather than through the game’s climactic church assault.
