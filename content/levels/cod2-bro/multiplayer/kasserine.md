## The Real Place & Differences

Kasserine is a real city in west-central Tunisia and the capital of Kasserine Governorate. It is distinct from **Kasserine Pass**, which lies west/northwest of the city and forms only part of the much larger February 1943 battlefield. U.S. Army histories and maps distinguish the settlement, the pass, and fighting farther west around the Bahiret Foussana valley, Bou Chebka, and Djebel el Hamra.
