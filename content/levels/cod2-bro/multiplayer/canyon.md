## The Real Mission & Differences

The closest supported historical context is **Operation Torch in French Algeria in November 1942**, especially the fighting around Oran. The U.S. Army's Center Task Force at Oran included the **1st Infantry Division**, the 1st Ranger Battalion, and Combat Command B of the 1st Armored Division. From 8 to 10 November, American forces fought Vichy French defenders around locations including Arzew, St. Cloud, Tafaraoui, La Sénia, and the approaches to Oran before the French cease-fire.

That history explains why a United States-versus-Vichy-France multiplayer pairing fits the game's North African setting, but it does **not** establish a real “Canyon” battle. The multiplayer map has no stated date or unit and no documented historical objective. Operation Torch and the Oran fighting are therefore context for the faction pairing, not a matching real mission.
