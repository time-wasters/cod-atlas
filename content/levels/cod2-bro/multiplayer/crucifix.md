## The Real Place & Differences

The strongest real-world anchor is **Haarberg (Hill 239)** in Aachen's Haaren district, near Verlautenheide. U.S. Army history places Crucifix Hill about 1,000 yards northwest of Verlautenheide and describes it as an exposed, pillbox-studded objective. The City of Aachen confirms that the summit carried the Haarener Kreuz; the original stone cross was destroyed during the October 1944 fighting and later replaced.

For the multiplayer map, this is best classified as a **plausible inspiration/analogue** rather than confirmed literal geography. The multiplayer article says “Eilendorf Ridge,” but also explicitly ties the map to *Crucifix Hill*. Historically, Crucifix Hill was Haarberg near Haaren and Verlautenheide, not a ridge in Eilendorf. The linked campaign level combines those locations, so the multiplayer setting inherits that fictionalization rather than establishing a separate exact place.

## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no standalone historical mission for the multiplayer map. The closest supported context is the real Battle of Crucifix Hill connected through the linked campaign level. On **8 October 1944**, the U.S. 1st Infantry Division's 18th Infantry Regiment attacked north of Aachen as part of the city's encirclement; Company C, 1st Battalion, under Captain Bobbie E. Brown, assaulted the fortified hill. Brown's actions there led to the Medal of Honor.
