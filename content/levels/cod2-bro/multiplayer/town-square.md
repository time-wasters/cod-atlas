## The Real Mission & Differences

The closest supported historical context is the **Battle of Troina**, fought as the U.S. 1st Infantry Division and attached Allied units attacked German and Italian defenses controlling the Troina ridge. On **August 5, 1943**, the 1st Division was still fighting mainly on the hills and approaches around the town: the 16th Infantry tried to advance against Hills 1034 and 1006 west of Troina without making headway, while other regiments fought north and south of the highway. German forces began withdrawing from Troina late that evening, and U.S. patrols entered the town on August 6 after the main withdrawal.
