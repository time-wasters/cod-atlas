The game places Private Martin with Baker Company, 506th Parachute Infantry
Regiment, 101st Airborne Division, on the outskirts of
Sainte-Mère-Église. His mission is to recover and activate a radio beacon
before joining the troops advancing toward the town.

Historically, the 101st Airborne's Drop Zone C lay north of Hiesville, between
Hiesville and Sainte-Marie-du-Mont. It was assigned to the 1st and 2nd
Battalions of the 506th PIR, together with the 3rd Battalion of the 501st PIR.
Pathfinder Team C was scheduled to jump shortly after midnight on June 6 and
establish its visual markers and Eureka radio beacon before the main airborne
serials arrived. This differs from the game's simplified 23:30 time and its
route directly toward Sainte-Mère-Église.

The marker uses the Lecaudey family farm at Hiesville, later the first Normandy
headquarters of 101st Airborne commander Major General Maxwell Taylor. The
[Cotentin tourist office](https://www.encotentin.fr/patrimoine-culturel/plaque-du-poste-de-commandement-du-general-m-taylor/)
records that the farmer's son helped a pathfinder install a beacon on the
farmhouse roof. [American War Memorials Overseas](https://www.uswarmemorials.org/html/site_details.php?SiteID=2397)
locates the surviving headquarters plaque at `49.371744, -1.262987`.

This is therefore an exact coordinate for a documented 101st Airborne
pathfinder-beacon site in the historical 506th PIR drop-zone area, not a claim
that the fictional mission reproduces this particular farm or route exactly.
