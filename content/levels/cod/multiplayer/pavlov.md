## The Mission in the Game

Pavlov has no mission narrative. It adapts **Pavlov's House**
(`cod-pavlov-s-house`) into a Soviet-versus-German multiplayer arena dominated
by the apartment building and open approaches.

## The Real Place & Differences

The real four-storey apartment block survives within post-war Volgograd. The
game makes it taller and rearranges its interior and surroundings for combat;
the modern building is integrated into a rebuilt residential block.

## The Real Mission & Differences

Members of the 42nd Guards Rifle Regiment held the building for weeks in 1942.
The short campaign assault and wave defence compress and dramatise that history;
multiplayer adds no further chronology.
