## The Mission in the Game

POW Camp has no mission narrative. It adapts the camp compound from **Dulag
IIIA** (`cod-dulag-iiia`) for American-versus-German multiplayer combat.

## The Real Place & Differences

The game's title and prisoner-rescue fiction conflate camp types. Strasshof was
a transit and forced-labour distribution camp, not the genuine Stalag III-A at
Luckenwalde. Its real grounds do not validate the map's compact barracks layout.

## The Real Mission & Differences

No historical 101st Airborne raid at Strasshof matches the campaign. On the
game's date the 506th PIR was fighting around Eindhoven; the multiplayer map
does not represent a separate real operation.

## Marker Position Explanation

`48.31581, 16.60989` marks the documented former Strasshof camp parcel and
memorial. Exact/high identifies the real site, not the fictional raid. Google
Maps searches for the memorial by name.
