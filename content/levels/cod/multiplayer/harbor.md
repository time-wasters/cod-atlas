## The Mission in the Game

Harbor has no mission narrative. Despite its English name, the original CoD
version is documented as a snowy Warsaw map derived from **Warsaw Factory**
(`cod-warsaw-factory`), with Soviet and German teams around industrial buildings.

## The Real Place & Differences

The former Ursus works is a plausible Warsaw analogue because occupying forces
used part of it as the Waverma armoured-vehicle repair plant. No evidence fixes
the game's geometry there, and “Harbor” should not be read as Warsaw waterfront.

## The Real Mission & Differences

German forces evacuated and prepared the works for destruction in 1944; local
Home Army action helped preserve it. This differs from the campaign's invented
17 January 1945 Soviet factory-clearing assault.
