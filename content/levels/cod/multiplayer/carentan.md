## The Real Mission & Differences

The closest real operation is the Battle of Carentan fought by the U.S. 101st Airborne Division to close the gap between the Utah and Omaha beachheads. U.S. Army University Press material states that VII Corps directed the 101st Airborne to capture the important road-junction city and consolidate the American lodgment. It describes the fight against the reinforced German 6th Fallschirmjäger Regiment through 12 June, followed by the German attempt to retake the city on 12-13 June using elements of the 17th SS Panzergrenadier Division.

The Army account places the decisive entry into Carentan on the morning of 12 June: 2nd Battalion, 506th Parachute Infantry entered from the southwest while 1st Battalion, 401st Glider Infantry drove in from the northeast, with the forces meeting after fighting around the railway-station area. On 13 June a German armored counterattack approached from the southwest and was repelled with the help of Combat Command A, 2nd Armored Division. This chronology is broader and more complex than the multiplayer arena, which has no historical sequence or operational objectives. The Wiki's 10-14 June date overlaps the real battle but should not be read as a literal duration for one street fight; Army University Press frames the main battle as 7-13 June, while the American Battle Monuments Commission timeline says the 101st finished liberating Carentan and completing the beachhead connection on 14 June.
