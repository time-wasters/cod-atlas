## The Mission in the Game

Ship has no mission narrative. It is a British-versus-German adaptation of
**Battleship Tirpitz** (`cod-battleship-tirpitz`), using the battleship's decks,
superstructure, bridge, stairs and interior passages as an arena.

## The Real Place & Differences

Tirpitz was a real German battleship, but the Wiki's vague “Atlantic Ocean”
label is less useful than her documented final anchorage off Håkøya. The game
compresses and simplifies a vast warship for infantry movement.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The campaign's disguised boarding raid is fictional. The closest real covert
attack was Operation Source by midget submarines at Kåfjord in 1943; RAF heavy
bombers sank Tirpitz off Håkøya on 12 November 1944.

## Marker Position Explanation

`69.647, 18.806` marks the surveyed final wreck area, not a moving ship at the
fictional raid time. Exact/high is supported for that historic site. Google
Maps searches for the Tirpitz wreck site.
