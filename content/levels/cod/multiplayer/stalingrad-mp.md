Stalingrad MP has no mission narrative. It combines material from **Red Square**
(`cod-red-square`) and **Stalingrad Sewers** (`cod-stalingrad-sewers`) into a
Soviet-versus-German urban arena above and below ground.

## The Real Place & Differences

The Square of the Fallen Fighters is the real central square called Red Square
during the battle. The multiplayer map composites it with an inferred sewer
route and does not reproduce modern or wartime Volgograd at survey accuracy.

## The Real Mission & Differences

square and station. Multiplayer objectives have no fixed date, unit or real
sequence and should not be treated as another historical mission.
