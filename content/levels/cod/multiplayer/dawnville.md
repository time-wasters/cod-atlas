## The Mission in the Game

Dawnville has no mission narrative. The original CoD layout adapts **Ste.
Mere-Eglise** (`cod-ste-mere-eglise`) and **Ste. Mere Eglise-Day**
(`cod-ste-mere-eglise-day`), centring combat on the church and surrounding town.

## The Real Place & Differences

Sainte-Mère-Église and its church are real and central to the D-Day airborne
story, but the compact multiplayer streets, accessible buildings and rail-side
combat are a gameplay reconstruction rather than a survey of the town.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The closest history is the 82nd Airborne Division's capture and defence of the
town on 6 June 1944, aided by scattered 101st troops. Multiplayer teams and
objectives do not reconstruct that chronology.

## Marker Position Explanation

`49.40882, -1.31626` marks the surviving parish church, the map's clearest
recognisable anchor. Exact/high applies to the landmark, not to every in-game
street. Google Maps searches for the church by name.
