## The Mission in the Game

**Brecourt is a multiplayer map and has no mission narrative.** Its large open
fields, central hedge, trenches, gun positions, farm and ruins support both
short-range trench fighting and longer sightlines. The original *Call of Duty*
and *United Offensive* versions share the same layout.

The map is explicitly based on the singleplayer **Brecourt Manor** level
(`cod-brecourt-manor`). The multiplayer article places it at Le Grand Chemin on
6 June 1944, but its repeatable American-versus-German matches are separate
from the campaign story.

## The Real Place & Differences

The identified place is the bocage farmland beside Brécourt Manor, south of Le
Grand Chemin in Sainte-Marie-du-Mont. A German artillery battery there fired
toward the Utah Beach exits on D-Day. The game's fields, hedges, linked
trenches and guns reflect the character of the site, although the multiplayer
layout enlarges and rearranges it for balanced movement and opposing spawns.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The related campaign level dramatizes the real assault led by Lieutenant
Richard Winters with men principally from E Company, 506th PIR, 101st Airborne
Division. They attacked four 105 mm howitzers and disabled them with
explosives; the game instead depicts FlaK 88 guns and fictional Baker Company
characters. The precise historical arrangement of the fourth gun remains
debated, so neither the campaign nor multiplayer trench plan should be treated
as a survey of the battlefield.
