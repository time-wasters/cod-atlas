## The Mission in the Game

Railyard has no mission narrative. The Warsaw map adapts **Warsaw Railyard**
(`cod-warsaw-railyard`), arranging freight cars, industrial buildings, elevated
firing positions and a tank depot around a central yard.

## The Real Place & Differences

The former Ursus works had documented freight sidings, making it a plausible
anchor for the campaign's factory-to-railyard sequence. The game layout is not
a verified plan of the historic siding, much of which later disappeared.

## The Real Mission & Differences

German forces had largely withdrawn when Polish and Soviet troops entered
Warsaw on 17 January 1945. The game's sustained factory-yard battle and unit
assignments are dramatic inventions rather than a documented action here.
