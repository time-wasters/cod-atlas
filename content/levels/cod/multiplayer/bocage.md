## The Mission in the Game

**Bocage is a multiplayer map and has no mission narrative.** Added to *Call
of Duty* in patch 1.3 and later included in *United Offensive*, `mp_bocage` is
a network of hedge-lined fields, connecting paths and defensive machine-gun
positions. A barn, wrecked Tiger I and crashed Horsa glider provide a broad
Second World War setting for the American and German teams.

No corresponding singleplayer mission is documented. It should not be
conflated with **Brecourt Manor** (`cod-brecourt-manor`), which has its own
separate multiplayer adaptation, **Brecourt** (`cod-brecourt`).

## The Real Place & Differences

“Bocage” is a landscape type rather than a unique place: small fields divided
by earth banks, ditches, trees and dense hedges, often connected by sunken
lanes. The map reproduces its enclosed sightlines and field-by-field movement.

The most plausible region is Normandy. U.S. Army research identifies extensive
hedgerow country around the Cotentin and the Coutances–Saint-Lô–Bayeux belt,
where this terrain strongly shaped the fighting. However, the map itself names
only France and contains no unique village, road or landmark. Its wartime props
cannot establish a particular battlefield.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The nearest historical context is the post-D-Day hedgerow fighting in western
Normandy, especially around Saint-Lô before Operation Cobra. The multiplayer
arena borrows that terrain and atmosphere but does not reconstruct the Battle
of Saint-Lô or any other documented operation.
