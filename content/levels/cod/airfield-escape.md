The Wiki identifies the setting only as an airfield in Kurhesse on September
2, 1944, directly after the Eder Dam escape. Fritzlar Air Base is the strongest
real-world match: it was a Luftwaffe airfield in historical Kurhessen, lies in
the Eder valley near the dam, and was active during 1944. Because the article
does not explicitly name Fritzlar, this marker records the exact real airfield
as a medium-confidence inspiration rather than a confirmed canonical location.
