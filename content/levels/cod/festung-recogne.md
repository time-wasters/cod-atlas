## Historical setting and location assessment

The mission identifies its setting as **Festung Recogne**, northeast of
Bastogne, and dates the action to 15 January 1945. The player fights as Private
Martin with Baker Company, 506th Parachute Infantry Regiment, 101st Airborne
Division. The objectives are to cross a mined field, enter two concrete
bunkers, recover German documents and repel a counterattack that includes two
Panzer IV tanks. The Call of Duty Wiki describes the fortress itself as
fictional; it also records `hurtgen` as the level's internal name and notes that
the battlefield was reused for the multiplayer map Hurtgen. Consequently, the
bunkers and their layout should not be treated as evidence for a particular
fortification at Recogne.

Recogne is nevertheless a genuine Battle of the Bulge location connected to
the 101st Airborne's defence of Bastogne. The U.S. Army's account of the battle
records fighting at Recogne on 20 December 1944: Company G held in the village
against German infantry and tanks, while the 3rd Battalion, 502nd Parachute
Infantry attacked through Recogne in an attempt to regain contact with the
American force at Noville. This establishes Recogne as a real battlefield on
the division's northern perimeter, but it does not substantiate the game's
fortress or its Baker Company raid.

The mission's exact date and regiment point to a different nearby action. A
506th PIR unit history places the regiment at Foy on 13 January, advancing
toward Cobru and the high ground around Noville on 14 January, and attacking
and capturing Noville on 15 January. Liberation Route Europe gives the same
broad sequence. Noville is therefore the closest documented match for what the
506th PIR was doing on the date shown in the mission, although the reviewed
sources do not identify an assault resembling the fictional bunker operation.
The game appears to combine the real Recogne place-name, the 506th's January
offensive north of Bastogne and a reused fictional battlefield.

The marker is placed at the centre of **Recogne village** because that is the
only location explicitly identified by the mission and historically verified
as a Bastogne battlefield. It replaces an earlier marker at the
Recogne-Bastogne German military cemetery. The Volksbund states that American
grave-registration troops began establishing that cemetery in February 1945;
it is an important memorial containing 6,807 German war dead, but it was not
the wartime fortress represented by the level. Village-level precision is used
because no evidence was found for an exact real-world counterpart to the
compound.

The wider chronology is also compressed in the game's framing. The encirclement
of Bastogne was broken on 26 December 1944, although the 101st Airborne
continued fighting in the area into mid-January. The mission is therefore
plausibly associated with the continuing Bastogne counteroffensive, not with
the still-unbroken siege implied by some of its introductory wording.
