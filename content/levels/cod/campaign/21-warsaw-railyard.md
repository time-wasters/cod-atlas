## The Mission in the Game

“Warsaw Railyard” begins at noon on January 17, 1945, immediately after
“Warsaw Factory,” which began at 10:30. Sergeant Alexei Voronin and the other
Soviet soldiers pass through the factory door directly into a yard containing
freight wagons. They cross it under friendly artillery fire, move through a
building holding tanks, and regroup with troops near a truck on the outskirts
of the factory complex. The mission is therefore a short continuation through
the same industrial site, not evidence for an independent main railway
station elsewhere in Warsaw.

## The Real Place & Differences

The historic factory siding beside the Ursus Mechanical Works at Czechowice
is the strongest real-world analogue. The factory stood beside the
Warsaw-Vienna railway. A passenger stop funded by the works opened there in
1926, and a dedicated siding was constructed beside it in 1929. Contemporary
local accounts also describe freight trains running on factory sidings to
industrial storage areas. An engineering report from the plant's construction
records that its earlier siding was extended and rebuilt to receive roughly
1,000 wagonloads of materials. The rails were consequently an integral part
of the complex long before the German occupation.

This pairing becomes particularly persuasive when considered with “Warsaw
Factory.” German forces converted the occupied Ursus works into Waverma, a
production-and-repair plant for tanks and aircraft pumps. A factory siding
capable of bringing freight wagons directly into a vehicle works explains the
game's adjacent railcars, workshops, and tank hall without moving the player
across the city between missions.

There are important limits to the identification. The historic siding is not
documented as a large classification yard and was removed in 1959, so its
precise wartime track arrangement is no longer visible. The game's compact
arena, elevated firing positions, armored vehicles, and artillery sequence
may be a fictional composition; no reviewed source shows that Infinity Ward
modelled the map on Ursus. Warsaw's much larger freight installations around
Odolany and the former Main Station are alternative visual matches, but they
fit the explicit factory-to-yard continuity less well.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The battle narrative also compresses and changes the history of January 17.
The Warsaw operation involved the Polish First Army and the Soviet 47th and
61st Armies. German covering troops offered limited resistance before
withdrawing to avoid encirclement, and contemporary historical summaries
place only sporadic fighting at a few locations. This does not support the
level's sustained battle by the 150th Rifle Division, nor its objective to
regroup with a “4th Army” at the complex. Those formations serve the game's
campaign continuity rather than a reliable order of battle for Warsaw.
