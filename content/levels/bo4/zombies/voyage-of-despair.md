## The Mission in the Game

Voyage of Despair depicts the RMS Titanic in the North Atlantic on April 14,
1912. The Chaos crew consists of Scarlett Rhodes, Diego Necalli, Bruno Delacroix,
and Stanton Shaw. Its fictional survival scenario involves an artifact and
undead enemies. The Beginner and Advanced Zombies tutorials reuse this map.

## The Real Place & Differences

The real ship's wreck lies south of Newfoundland's Grand Banks. NOAA's 2004
expedition instructions locate the bow section near 41 degrees 43 minutes
57 seconds north, 49 degrees 56 minutes 49 seconds west, at about 3,800 metres
depth. The game depicts a playable surface ship, not this underwater ruin.
The wreck site is a geographic anchor for the depicted vessel; it does not
establish the exact position of the iceberg collision or the game's ship.

## The Real Mission & Differences

Titanic struck an iceberg on the night of April 14, 1912, and sank early on
April 15. The game's supernatural combat and crew objectives are fictional,
not a reconstruction of a historical military operation.
