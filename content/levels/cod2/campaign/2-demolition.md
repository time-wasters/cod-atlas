## The Mission in the Game

*Demolition* is the second Soviet mission of *Call of Duty 2*. Private Vasili
Ivanovich Koslov serves with the 13th Guards Rifle Division in Stalingrad on
December 2, 1942. His diary says that the division has suffered heavily since
crossing the Volga in September, and the level caption places the action
**three blocks north of Solechnaya Street**.

Koslov's squad clears a building, crosses a railway, and reinforces a Soviet
position against a German attack. The soldiers then assault a fortified
building flying a Nazi flag. When Germans barricade themselves upstairs,
Lieutenant Leonov orders the structure destroyed; Koslov obtains explosives,
plants charges on the lower floor, and withdraws before the blast. The mission
ends with Commissar Letlev and leads into *Repairing the Wire*
(`cod2-repairing-the-wire`).

## The Real Place & Differences

The level's “Solechnaya” is best understood as a transliteration or spelling
variant of **Solnechnaya Street** (Солнечная улица, “Sunny Street”), a real
street in central wartime Stalingrad. Volgograd's municipal street register
records Solnechnaya as the former name of today's **13th Guards Division
Street**. The modern name is especially relevant because it commemorates the
same division represented in the mission. The surviving street is in central
Volgograd between the railway-side city blocks and Lenin Avenue, close to the
division's historical bridgehead near the Volga.

This establishes a defensible neighbourhood, not the mission's exact route.
The current street plan and rebuilt city cannot be matched block for block to
the ruined level, and no reviewed source identifies the German headquarters,
the buildings Koslov clears, or the particular railway crossing. Historical
maps and aerial photography confirm that the central district was densely
built and crossed by rail infrastructure, but they do not demonstrate that the
game reproduces one surviving structure. The level is therefore best treated
as a fictional or composite slice of central Stalingrad anchored to a genuine
wartime street.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The unit and broad situation are historical. Major General Alexander
Rodimtsev's 13th Guards Rifle Division crossed the Volga on the nights of
September 14–16, 1942 and fought for the central landing, Stalingrad-1 railway
station, Mamayev Kurgan, and a narrow belt of strongpoints along the western
bank. Divisional histories and position maps show it still holding the city
centre through November 27–December 4, so its presence near Solnechnaya Street
on the game's date is plausible. Koslov's recollection of severe losses during
the September crossing also reflects a documented danger: boats and barges
were exposed to German artillery and mortar fire.

By December 2, however, the strategic situation was different from the
mission's self-contained counterattack. Operation Uranus had closed the ring
around the German Sixth Army on November 23. Fighting and local attacks
continued inside Stalingrad, and the 13th Guards retained its constricted
defensive sector, but no reviewed operational record ties December 2 to the
destruction of a German headquarters three blocks north of Solnechnaya Street.
The explosives run, named Soviet characters, and collapse of that building
should consequently be read as dramatized gameplay rather than a reconstruction
of a documented action. The mission's genuine unit, street reference, and
urban battlefield have been combined with an unverified tactical episode.
