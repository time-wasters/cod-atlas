## The Mission in the Game

*City Hall* continues immediately after *Downtown Assault*
(`cod2-downtown-assault`). Private Vasili Ivanovich Koslov and Lieutenant
Dimitri Volsky's 13th Guards Rifle Division squad are still advancing through
central Stalingrad on January 15, 1943. Koslov's diary entry is timed at 13:40;
the playable action begins at 15:00 in heavy snow and extreme cold.

The squad initially hides while a German armoured convoy passes because it has
no anti-tank weapons. Volsky then leads the soldiers through ruined streets and
a square containing a monument. They fight past a disabled streetcar, clear an
MG42 position, enter the building behind it, and blow open a metal interior
door. The mission ends at that breach rather than after the whole building has
been secured.

The next mission, *Comrade Sniper* (`cod2-comrade-sniper`), resumes inside the
same supposed City Hall. It contains the west-wing clearance, sniper duel, and
defence against a German counterattack. Those events are therefore related to
this location but are not objectives of *City Hall* itself.

## The Real Place & Differences

The most plausible real-world area is the **Square of the Fallen Fighters**,
wartime Stalingrad's central civic square. Government offices, hotels, the
Central Department Store, the Gorky Theatre, and other public buildings stood
around or near it, while tram routes served the central district. Its civic
character, monument, dense surrounding streets, and proximity to the final
German southern pocket fit the level better than a generic point elsewhere in
modern Volgograd.

This does not identify one real building as the game's City Hall. Period
photographs document a building labelled the Stalingrad City Council, and the
square's former Tsaritsyn municipal building had become a theatre long before
the battle. Several other regional and municipal offices also occupied the
centre. No reviewed source connects the in-game exterior or floor plan to any
one of them, and the mission never supplies a street address or official
building name. “City Hall” is therefore best understood as a convenient game
label for a composite civic building.

The modern square cannot be used as a direct architectural comparison. Most of
central Stalingrad was ruined in the battle and the post-war reconstruction
enlarged the square and replaced nearly all of its surrounding buildings. The
marker represents the historic civic sector, not the footprint of a surviving
City Hall.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The winter setting belongs broadly to Operation Ring, the Soviet offensive
launched on January 10, 1943 to eliminate the encircled German Sixth Army. The
game's ruined streets and block-by-block resistance are credible for the final
phase of the battle, but its date, unit, and specific assault are not a close
historical reconstruction.

On January 15 the main Soviet forces advancing from the west had not yet
captured central Stalingrad. The 13th Guards and other 62nd Army formations
linked with those forces only on January 26. The German southern pocket then
made the Square of the Fallen Fighters and its surrounding buildings a major
strongpoint. The documented final advance into the square was made chiefly by
the 38th Motorized Rifle Brigade of the 64th Army on January 30–31. Its troops
took buildings including the Regional Executive Committee and Stalingrad Hotel
and surrounded the Central Department Store, where Field Marshal Friedrich
Paulus and his headquarters surrendered.

No reviewed historical source describes Volsky and Koslov's route, a 13th
Guards assault on a City Hall on January 15, or the exact monument–streetcar–
MG42 sequence. The level appears to move imagery from the real late-January
battle for the civic centre about two weeks earlier and assign it to the game's
recurring Soviet unit.

The marker at `48.708028, 44.515442` is placed in the Square of the Fallen
Fighters. This is the best-supported representative anchor for wartime
Stalingrad's civic centre and the final battles around its administrative
buildings, but it is not asserted to be the doorway breached in the game.
