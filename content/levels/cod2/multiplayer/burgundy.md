The geographic region broadly corresponds to the former administrative region made up of Côte-d'Or, Nièvre, Saône-et-Loire and Yonne, with Dijon as its historic administrative capital. The former region was merged into Bourgogne-Franche-Comté in 2016, but “Burgundy” remains a well-defined historical and geographic name.
