Decoytown has no multiplayer mission to match to a real operation. The closest directly relevant historical event is the **Battle of Alam el Halfa**, fought from 30 August to 5 September 1942. The 7th Armoured Division operated on the southern open flank, while other British formations held or reinforced the ridge itself. This provides authentic historical context for British-versus-German combat around the named feature, but it does not document a battle for a compact village resembling Decoytown.
