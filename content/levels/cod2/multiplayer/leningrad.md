The game's place name refers to wartime **Leningrad**, the city now named **Saint Petersburg**. The Saint Petersburg city administration records that the city was called Leningrad from 1924 until its historical name was restored in 1991. ## The Real Mission & Differences

The closest historical context is the **Siege of Leningrad**, which lasted from 8 September 1941 to 27 January 1944. German and Finnish forces isolated the city while Soviet forces defended Leningrad and its approaches. The city endured artillery fire, air attack, starvation, and extreme winter conditions.

A major difference from the multiplayer premise is that German ground forces did not fight their way through central Leningrad in the manner suggested by a Red Army-versus-German urban arena. The Central Naval Museum states that the city's defenders prevented enemy land forces from entering Leningrad. The closest sustained ground fighting occurred on the approaches, defensive lines, and later breakthrough sectors rather than as a conventional German occupation battle inside the city centre.

Because the multiplayer map gives no date or named operation, it should not be assigned to Operation Iskra, the final Leningrad-Novgorod offensive, or any other particular action. Its historical value is as a stylized evocation of besieged wartime Leningrad, not as a reconstruction of one documented engagement.
