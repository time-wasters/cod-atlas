## The Mission in the Game

El Alamein is a **multiplayer map**, so it has no playable campaign character, mission date, briefing, route, or historical objective of its own. *Call of Duty 2* places the map at **El Alamein, Egypt**, with British forces fighting German forces. Its internal console name is `mp_decoy`. The playable space is a night-time desert trench network with dugouts and bunkers; Call of Duty Maps also notes open desert beyond the combat area, rocky high ground, and mine warnings around the perimeter.

The campaign relationship is unusually explicit. The Call of Duty Wiki identifies **The Diversionary Raid** (`cod2-the-diversionary-raid`) as El Alamein's campaign map, while the campaign article reciprocally lists El Alamein as its multiplayer map. The singleplayer mission uses the internal name `decoytrenches` and follows Sergeant John Davis of the British 7th Armoured Division on 29 October 1942. Its gameplay caption places the action **25 miles south of El Alamein**, where the squad attacks a fictional Axis supply dump through trenches and bunkers before destroying fuel and ammunition stores and recovering documents. This documented relationship is strong enough to use the campaign mission's geography when choosing the multiplayer marker, but it does not prove that every multiplayer trench, bunker, or route is a one-to-one copy of the singleplayer space.

## The Real Place & Differences

**Match classification: plausible real-world inspiration/analogue.** The strongest real-world anchor is **Qaret el Himeimat**, a prominent hill at the southern end of the El Alamein battlefield near the northern edge of the Qattara Depression. The hill lies about 27 miles south of the modern El Alamein locality, close to the linked campaign mission's stated 25-mile distance. Historical map records also document wartime defences around El Himeimat, while modern gazetteers independently identify Qaret el Himeimat as a named hill in Matrouh Governorate.

The broad setting fits the linked mission better than central El Alamein does. The real southern battlefield was open desert broken by low hills, escarpments, tracks, minefields, trenches, and dispersed field positions. Qaret el Himeimat was a conspicuous terrain feature at the end of the Alamein line where the Qattara Depression constrained movement. These characteristics are compatible with the multiplayer map's trenches, dugouts, mine warnings, and rocky desert backdrop.

No reviewed source, however, identifies the multiplayer map's trench geometry, dugouts, bunkers, intact gun positions, or fictional supply area with a specific surviving position on Qaret el Himeimat. The map is therefore best treated as an adapted combat space inspired by the southern Alamein battlefield rather than a reconstruction of a documented site.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The closest historical context comes from the **7th Armoured Division's role on the southern flank of the Second Battle of El Alamein**. The Desert Rats Association describes Montgomery's main attack as being in the north while the 7th Armoured and 44th Divisions of XIII Corps carried out a diversionary attack just north of the Himeimat ridge. The Queen's Royal Hussars Museum likewise records that the 7th Armoured Division's role in the southern sector was to contain the 21st Panzer Division while operations around Himeimat and the minefields attempted to fix Axis forces away from the main northern effort.

That historical setting supports the campaign's broad ideas of diversion, minefields, and fighting in the southern Alamein sector, but it does **not** establish the small supply-depot raid depicted by The Diversionary Raid. The real action involved formation-level attacks and containment across a wide battlefield, not a documented infantry mission to destroy fuel barrels, ammunition stores, and an intelligence bunker. The southern Axis defences also included major Italian formations, especially the Folgore Division, so both the campaign mission and the multiplayer map simplify the opposing forces by presenting the combat chiefly as British versus German.
