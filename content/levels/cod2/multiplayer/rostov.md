## The Real Place & Differences

Rostov-on-Don is a real port city on the Don River. The city's official tourism history describes its development around major land and water routes and a longstanding commercial port. The modern Rostov Port operator identifies Cargo Area No. 1 at Beregovaya Street 30 on the right bank of the Don. That central port area is a good real-world analogue for the map's combined harbor, factory, and river cues.

The match is best classified as a **plausible inspiration/analogue**. ## The Real Mission & Differences

There is no multiplayer mission to match to a historical operation. The version 1.2 patch notes associate the setting with the November 1941 Battle of Rostov: German forces captured Rostov, after which a Soviet counteroffensive involving the 37th Army forced the overextended German First Panzer Army to withdraw. This is broad operational context, not evidence for a discrete harbor action corresponding to the multiplayer map.

Local Rostov historical material adds that Soviet forces and the Rostov militia crossed the Don during the city's liberation and that the 56th Army participated in the fighting. The real battle therefore extended across approaches to the city, river crossings, and urban districts. No evidence was found for a specific 37th-Army engagement at the selected port cargo area that matches the compact `mp_harbor` arena, so the multiplayer fighting should not be read as a reconstruction of a documented port skirmish.
