## The Mission in the Game

*Wallendar* is a **multiplayer map**, so it has no playable campaign character,
unit, date, briefing, route, or historical objective of its own. The Call of
Duty Wiki places it at “Wallendar, Germany,” with the United States Army
fighting German forces, and gives its console codename as `mp_rhine`. Call of
Duty Maps describes a compact German town of tight streets and numerous
buildings used as flank routes.

The campaign relationship is explicit: the Wiki identifies **The Crossing
Point**, presented in the campaign as *Crossing the Rhine*
(`cod2-crossing-the-rhine`), as Wallendar's campaign map. That singleplayer
level uses the related console name `rhine` and places Corporal Bill Taylor of
D Company, 2nd Ranger Battalion at “Wallendar, Germany” on 24 March 1945. Its
gameplay begins with an amphibious Rhine crossing and continues through the
town against gun positions and tanks. The multiplayer documentation does not
establish which individual streets, buildings, or combat encounters are copied
one-to-one, so the safest description is a documented multiplayer counterpart
to the campaign's Wallendar combat space rather than a separately evidenced
historical reconstruction.

## The Real Mission & Differences

The linked campaign level supplies the closest historical comparison. Its
fictional D Company attacks across the Rhine directly into Wallendar on
24 March 1945 so engineers can establish a bridgehead. The historical chronology
is different. The U.S. Army Center of Military History records the First Army
breakout from the Remagen bridgehead beginning on **25 March**, with the 9th
Armored Division advancing down the **east bank** of the Rhine as far as
Vallendar. A local Vallendar wartime chronicle likewise records American troops
entering on 25 March from Bendorf and Weitersburg rather than landing directly
across the river.

There is also a genuine 2nd Ranger Battalion connection, but not the exact
mission depicted by the game. A Company C history reproduced by the U.S. Army
Special Operations History Office states that the battalion was attached to
the 102nd Cavalry Group, with Companies D, E, and F working with the 102nd
Cavalry. It records Company C crossing the Rhine at Honnigen on **26 March**,
then proceeding to Vallendar and relieving the 27th Armored Infantry Battalion
on high ground. This supports Vallendar as a historically meaningful Ranger
location, while showing that the game's March 24 direct amphibious assault
compresses and rearranges the real chronology. No reliable evidence was found
for a historical D Company assault matching the campaign's exact river landing,
gun positions, tank fight, or engineer objective.
